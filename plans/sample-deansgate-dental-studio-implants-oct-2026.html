Questions

Dental implant FAQs

General information only. Your dentist will advise on your own situation at consultation.

How long does implant treatment take?

It varies from patient to patient. Implant treatment usually happens in stages over several months, because the implant needs time to settle before the final tooth or teeth are fitted. Your dentist will give you an expected timeline in your treatment plan.

Is having a dental implant painful?

The procedure is carried out under local anaesthetic, so the area is numbed. Some tenderness and swelling afterwards is common for a few days. Your dentist will explain what to expect and how to manage it.

What affects the price of implant treatment?

The number of teeth being replaced, the type of restoration (a single crown, a supported denture or a retained denture), whether extra procedures are needed first, and whether you choose sedation. Our published fees start from £2500, and your exact fee is confirmed after consultation.

I am nervous about dental treatment. Can I have sedation?

IV sedation is available from £400. Whether it is suitable for you depends on your medical history, which the dentist will check with you at consultation.

How do I get started?

The first step is a consultation. Use the form on this page, book online, message on WhatsApp or call 0161 912 6200. The dentist will assess whether implants are suitable for you and talk you through the options.
