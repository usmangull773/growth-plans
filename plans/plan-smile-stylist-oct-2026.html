1

One fast Invisalign landing page per ad group

Each Google ad group and Meta campaign gets its own page: gaps, crowding, crooked teeth and relapse after braces. Each shows a from price, a monthly finance example, your reviews and awards, and the free consultation and 3D scan above the fold, built to score 80 or higher on mobile. A sample Invisalign landing page is included with this plan.

Why: a page that matches the search and shows a price turns more of the same clicks into consultations.
2

One booking route with tracking across both clinics

Every button on the paid pages goes to one booking tool, with the clinic chosen inside it. Bookings, form fills, calls and WhatsApp taps are tracked as conversions in Google Ads, Meta and GA4, tagged by clinic and treatment.

Why: one route means one diary, one report and a clear answer to which ad produced the patient.
3

Automate the follow-up

Instant reply to every enquiry, SMS and email reminders before the consultation, a no-show rebook sequence, and a follow-up for treatment plans given but not yet started, with the finance option restated.

Why: the cheapest new patient is the enquiry you already paid for.
4

Scale Google and Meta on real outcomes

Import consultation attended and treatment started as offline conversions, then move budget towards the ad groups, audiences and clinic that produce started treatments, not just form fills.

Why: bidding that learns from treatment starts finds more patients like the ones who say yes.
5

One weekly patient report

Enquiries, booked consultations, attended and treatment started, by treatment, clinic and channel. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
