01

What we found

The production side is strong: national brand work, a clear process, a published price floor and a GoHighLevel landing page already live. The gap is on the buying side. Search for "video production" mixes $5,000 corporate buyers with students, job seekers and $300 wedding shoots, and right now nothing in the funnel separates them.

1. The keyword pool decides lead quality

High impact

Broad video terms pull in people who will never spend $5,000. The buyers you want search by video type and business use, such as corporate, commercial, training or recruitment video.

2. The form cannot tell a $5,000 project from a $500 one

High impact

landing.thinksparkinc.com asks for name, email, phone and business name only. Google and LinkedIn will optimize toward whoever fills that form, qualified or not. Three extra questions fix this and give bidding a value signal.

Medium impact

1

Build tracking that counts projects, not form fills

GA4 and GTM on the landing page, call tracking, and the three qualifying fields added to the GoHighLevel form. Budget band becomes the conversion value. Qualified leads and booked sessions get uploaded back to Google as offline conversions.

Why: bidding learns from $5,000+ inquiries instead of any form submit.

2

Launch Jacksonville Search by video type

Separate ad groups for corporate, commercial and TV, training and internal, recruitment, and industry terms (healthcare, legal, higher ed). Exact and phrase match, with a negative list covering weddings, jobs, schools, DIY and editing software from day one.

Why: each ad and landing section matches what the buyer typed.

3

Use your own reels as the ad creative

Ad copy leads with the $5,000 starting price, no travel fees and 3 to 4 week delivery. Sitelinks point to industry sections. Video assets come from your portfolio, which most competing agencies cannot match.

Why: a visible price floor filters out small budget clicks before you pay for them.

4

Start LinkedIn with job title targeting

Marketing directors, CMOs, HR and talent leaders, and communications managers at 50 to 1,000 employee companies in Jacksonville. Video ads cut from your reels, with a LinkedIn lead form that asks the same budget and timeline questions.

Why: LinkedIn reaches corporate buyers before they start searching.

5

Read week 4 data, then test one new city

Review search terms, cost per qualified lead and budget split. If Jacksonville impression share is already high, open Orlando or Tampa with the best performing ad groups only.

Why: expansion should follow the data, not the other way round.
