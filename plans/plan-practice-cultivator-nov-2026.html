01

What we found

Your Google Ads strategy is already right: high intent Search, niche keywords, no broad match autopilot. The gap is underneath it. Google is being trained on leads, while you are judged on paying clients, and the data that connects the two lives in a different system on almost every account.

1. Tracking is rebuilt one site at a time, so every account reports a different version of "a lead"

High impact

Your public project history shows separate jobs for Enhanced Conversions (twice), UTM capture on a Webflow form, a Zoho two step form with Ads tracking, and a GoHighLevel contact form. Each fix was sensible on its own. Together they mean there is no single tracking standard across the portfolio.

Root cause: client sites run on different builders and CRMs, and tracking gets patched when something breaks instead of being installed from one template. Effect: bidding on each account learns from a slightly different signal, and month to month comparisons across clients are unreliable.

2. Bidding sees the inquiry, not the consult or the paying client

High impact

Your post asks for "leads that turn into paying clients" and for someone to "analyze CRM and lead quality data". That tells us the CRM already knows which leads booked, and Google Ads does not. Until booked consults flow back into Google Ads, Smart Bidding will keep favoring the cheapest form fill.

3. Small budgets mean thin data, so each account needs the whole portfolio's learning

Medium impact

At $1,500 to $3,000 a month and therapy click costs often in double digits, one account sees a limited number of clicks. That is too little data for any single account to learn fast on its own. The advantage you have is 12 accounts searching for the same niches: EMDR, trauma, couples, anxiety, teens.

02

The 30 day plan

Same budgets, same strategy, no broad match and no Performance Max. The first month is about making Google see what you see, then letting the accounts scale on it.

Week by week, first 6 accounts

Week 1

Audit and baseline

  • Review conversions, call length rules and Enhanced Conversions on all 6 accounts
  • 90 day search term review, first shared negative list
  • Pull lead to consult to client rate from the CRM

You get: a one page audit per account and the first Loom update.

Week 2

One tracking standard

  • Same GTM template on every site builder
  • GCLID captured in a hidden field on every form
  • Calls over 60 seconds set as the primary call conversion

You get: every account counting a lead the same way.

Week 3

CRM feeds Google Ads

  • Import "Consult booked" and "Became client" from GoHighLevel or Zoho, with values
  • Niche ad group templates, exact and phrase match only
  • Landing page briefs for your copywriter and developer

You get: bidding that learns from paying clients, not form fills.

Week 4

Report and scale

  • Looker Studio report: spend, inquiries, consults, new clients, cost per client
  • Budget moved toward the niches producing clients
  • Next month's tests and new niche ideas per account

You get: cost per paying client on every account, and a scale plan.

1

Audit every account against one checklist (week 1)

  • Primary vs secondary conversions, call length thresholds, Enhanced Conversions status
  • Search terms for the last 90 days, match types, location setting (presence only)
  • Lead to consult to client rate from the CRM, per niche

Why: you get a written baseline per account before anything changes, so every later result is provable.

2

Install one tracking standard and connect the CRM (weeks 1 to 3)

  • One GTM template, GCLID stored in a hidden field on every form, whatever the site builder
  • Offline import of "Consult booked" and "Became client" from GoHighLevel or Zoho, with values
  • Inquiry stays as a secondary signal so data volume does not collapse

Why: bidding starts favoring the searches that turn into paying clients, which is the metric you are measured on.

3

Build a shared negative and keyword library (weeks 1 to 4)

  • Portfolio negative lists: therapist jobs and training, free and student clinics, worksheets, crisis lines, insurance mismatch terms per client
  • Niche ad group templates (EMDR, trauma, couples, anxiety, teen) with exact and phrase match only

Why: one bad query found on one account is blocked on all twelve the same day.

4

Brief your copywriter and developer on the pages that leak (weeks 3 to 4)

  • Message match: the ad's niche and promise appear above the fold on the page it lands on
  • Booking path: fee, insurance and online or in person answered before the form, not after
  • Speed to lead check: how fast forms get a reply and how many calls go unanswered

Why: the copy and design work your team already does gets aimed at the exact pages losing consults.

5

Weekly report built around paying clients (every week)

  • One Looker Studio template: spend, inquiries, consults, new clients, cost per client
  • A short Loom per account: what changed, what worked, what is next, flagged problems first

Why: you and your clients see progress without asking, and scale decisions are based on clients, not clicks.
