01

What the role needs

You want someone who builds the strategy, implements it, tracks everything correctly and talks to clients with confidence. In an agency, that comes down to one repeatable operating standard applied to every account, so results do not depend on who happens to be looking that week.

1. E-commerce and lead gen accounts need different rules

High impact

Your case studies cover both: online stores scaled on Shopping and Performance Max, and dealerships, clinics and service firms paying per lead. Running both well means separate conversion goals, bid strategies and report KPIs, set from day one rather than copied between accounts.

Account typePrimary conversionBiddingKPI in the weekly report
E-commercePurchase with real order valueTarget ROAS by product groupRevenue, ROAS, new customer share
Lead generationQualified lead or booked callTarget CPA, then value based once lead quality is fed backCost per qualified lead, lead volume
Local servicesCalls over a set duration and quote formsTarget CPA by locationCost per call, answer rate

2. Every report is only as good as the tracking under it

High impact

The fastest way to lose a client is a dashboard that disagrees with their sales. Each account gets the same tracking check before any optimisation starts, and the same setup becomes the onboarding template for every new client you sign.

CheckCommon gap in inherited accountsStandard I set
Conversion actionsPage views and clicks counted as conversionsOnly sales, qualified leads and real calls as primary
GA4 and GTMDuplicate tags, missing key eventsOne container, documented events
Enhanced conversionsNot switched onLive on every account
Consent Mode v2 (EU)Missing, so EU conversions are lostSet up with a consent banner
Offline and CRM dataLead quality never reaches GoogleOffline conversion import where a CRM exists

Audit every account and rank them by risk

Tracking, structure, search terms, budget pacing and policy issues checked per account, so the first fixes go where the most client money is at stake.

Week 1

Bring every account up to one tracking standard

Clean primary conversions, enhanced conversions and Consent Mode v2, so bidding learns from sales and qualified leads instead of clicks.

Restructure the weakest accounts first

Search split by intent, Shopping and Performance Max on clean product feeds for e-commerce, shared negative lists. Budget moves to whatever converts cheapest.

One landing page test per lead gen account

Form length, offer above the fold and call tracking. On a UK aesthetics clinic, a new landing page drove 10 of 11 leads in its first full month.

One page each week and a Looker Studio dashboard per client. You stay informed without having to manage the detail.
