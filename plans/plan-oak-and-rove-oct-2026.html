01

What we found

From the public site and the brief, three things decide whether Google is producing incremental profitable demand or reporting sales that would have happened anyway. Each is checkable in the first two days of account access.

Product-name searches can look like non-brand acquisition

High impact

"Alto briefcase" and "Kennedy weekender" are searched by people who already read the GQ or Wired review. Unless those names sit in the brand bucket, Shopping and PMax will report them as cold traffic and platform ROAS will overstate true acquisition.

Stock status and ad spend may be out of step

High impact

Both hero bag pages currently say "Item Currently Out of Stock. Estimated Delivery: Feb. 24-28", next to a sale banner. Shopping and PMax spend follows the feed, so this needs to match before Black Friday.

What a shopper sees now

  • Sale banner: $50 off Alto, $125 off Kennedy
  • Same page: out of stock, February delivery
  • Same page: "Orders Ship 24 - 48 Hours"

What the account should do

  • Feed availability set to preorder or backorder with an availability date, matching the page
  • Bids on hero bags set for preorder conversion rate, not in-stock rate
  • Budget shifted to in-stock items until restock lands

One blended ROAS target hides very different margins

Medium impact

Discount depth ranges from 0% to almost 24% across four products. A single ROAS target will overfund the deepest discount and underfund products that bring in a first order at full price.

Measurement first, structure second, scale last. Nothing scales until brand and non-brand are reported separately.

1
Week 1

Build the brand list and split reporting

Brand, product names and misspellings go into one shared list, applied as PMax brand exclusions and Shopping negatives, so non-brand numbers finally mean non-brand.

2
Week 1

Check that purchase value matches what you keep

Confirm the purchase conversion sends value after discount, turn on new customer reporting, and reconcile Google against Shopify orders, because bidding can only aim at the number it is given.

3
Week 1 to 2

Fix the feed: stock, preorder dates and margin labels

Availability matches the page, and custom labels by margin tier and new customer value let each product carry its own target instead of one blended ROAS.

4
Week 2 to 3

Give each campaign type one job

Standard Shopping for query-level non-brand control, PMax for non-brand reach with brand excluded, Search split into brand (capped) and category non-brand, so spend can be traced to new demand.

5
Week 3 to 4

Scale on contribution profit, then test incrementality

Budget rises only where non-brand contribution profit holds. A brand-search geo holdout shows how much branded spend is incremental before Q4 budgets increase.
