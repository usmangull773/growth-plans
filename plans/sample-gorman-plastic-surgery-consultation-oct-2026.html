Consultation questions

Quick answers to what patients ask most. Mr Gorman will go through your own case in person.

Who will I see at my consultation?

You will see Mr Mark Gorman himself. He will talk through your goals, examine you where needed and explain the options that suit you.

Do I have to decide on the day?

No. You will leave with a clear plan and time to think it over. A cooling off period applies before any cosmetic surgery, and you can book a second consultation if you have more questions.

Can I have more than one procedure at once?

Some patients combine procedures, for example breast surgery with a tummy tuck as part of a mummy makeover. Whether that is right for you is discussed at your consultation.

Where does surgery take place?

Mr Gorman primarily practises at Pines Hospital in Wythenshawe, Manchester. Where your surgery takes place will be confirmed in your written plan.

What aftercare is included?

Patients describe follow-up phone calls and clinic appointments during recovery. The exact aftercare for your procedure will be set out in your written plan.
