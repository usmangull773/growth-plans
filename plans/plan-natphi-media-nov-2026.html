What we found

Your pricing pages and job brief tell the same story. Clients pay weekly for active management they can feel, and the founders need someone who owns the accounts so they can keep selling and onboarding.

Your packages promise weekly work. The risk is a portfolio that only gets touched monthly.

High impact

Growth, Advanced and Elite all include search term and negative keyword work, budget and bidding changes, ad testing and a dedicated account manager. On a weekly fee, a quiet account is the fastest route to churn. The fix is a fixed weekly owner cycle per account, with a short written log your Client Manager can forward or quote.

The 30 day plan

Week one is about knowing every account. By week four the portfolio runs on a rhythm your Client Manager can rely on without chasing.

1

Portfolio audit and one page account sheet

Every account reviewed for tracking, policy, billing, budget pacing, cost per lead trend and the top three issues. One sheet per client, shared with the Client Manager.

Why: you get a full picture of risk and upside in the first week.

2

Tracking fixed before bidding is trusted

Primary conversions cleaned, call length rules set, GTM form triggers checked, GA4 imports de-duplicated.

Why: Smart Bidding only gets better when it learns from real leads.

3

Search terms and shared negatives by vertical

Shared negative lists for trades, health and professional services, then account specific lists on top. Jobs, DIY, free and wrong-suburb queries removed.

Why: new accounts start clean instead of paying for the same waste.

4

Structure and budget moved to what converts

Ad groups split by service where intent differs, Performance Max given brand exclusions and service specific asset groups, budget moved to the lowest cost per lead.

Why: the same spend produces more calls and forms.

5

Build template for new clients

A standard build for each of your plans: keyword research, campaign layout, tracking checklist and launch QA.

Why: new accounts go live faster and built the same way every time.

6

Weekly internal update

Each Friday: what changed, why, concerns, what is next, and the one number to watch per account.

Why: your Client Manager never has to ask what is happening.

Weekly cycle for one lead gen account

  1. Monday: health check. Spend pacing, disapprovals, billing, tag status, conversion volume against last week.
  2. Tuesday: search terms. Add negatives, promote converting queries to exact or phrase keywords.
  3. Wednesday: performance by campaign, ad group and keyword. Cut what spends without leads, push budget to cheapest leads.
  4. Thursday: ads and assets. Pause losing variants, write new tests, check landing page speed and form.
  5. Friday: written update to the Client Manager and change log saved.
