01

What we found

You already have the hard parts: real med spa ranking wins, a clear price, a named Growth Manager model and a sales team being built. The gap is that paid traffic has no single path to follow, and Google cannot see which clicks become booked and closed calls. Fix those two things and Google Ads works the way you want it to: fast and measurable.

2. Google only sees the click, not the booked call or the close

High impact

Bookings happen inside Typeform, Calendly and Stripe. Unless the click ID is passed through and the outcome is sent back, Smart Bidding optimises toward form opens, not qualified calls. With a new appointment setter also adding outbound leads to the pipeline, inbound and outbound will blur together unless every lead carries its source.

1

Week 1: Build the measurement spine

  • GA4 and GTM audit, then server side tagging and Consent Mode v2
  • Capture gclid, gbraid and UTMs into Typeform and Calendly hidden fields, and pass them into the CRM
  • Enhanced conversions for leads (hashed email and phone)
  • CRM stages: Lead, Booked, Held, Qualified, Proposal, Won. Each lead gets a "source" field so outbound setter leads never mix with paid leads
  • Offline conversion import from the CRM to Google Ads, with a value on each stage
  • GA4 channel for AI assistant referrals (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini), so paid and AI search are reported side by side

Why: Smart Bidding learns from what you feed it. Feed it qualified calls and closed plans, and it finds more owners who buy.

2

Week 2: Two paid landing pages, built on your stack

  • Med spa page: headline "Get your med spa into the Google Maps top 3 and ChatGPT answers". Above the fold: the Southlake, Tampa and Midland Maps wins. Offer: a free live 104 point check of their site. Booking embedded on the page, not a redirect
  • AI search page: "Is ChatGPT recommending your competitor?" Offer: an AI visibility snapshot for their business and three local rivals
  • One price story only ($2,950 build or $3,500 existing site), a short FAQ, the Growth Manager photo and the comparison table you already have
  • Three qualifying questions in the booking form (business type, locations, monthly revenue band) so the setter and closers only take fit calls

Why: you said you can build great page experiences. The ads only need a focused page and an offer worth a click. Your 104 point check is that offer.

3

Week 3: Launch tightly controlled Search

  • Three campaigns: Med Spa, Clinics, AI Search. Exact and phrase match only to start
  • US targeting, schedule aligned to your sales team hours, call assets and lead form assets on
  • Responsive search ads with ad copy pinned to the page headline. Use proof lines like "#1 on Maps for 36 searches"
  • Negative keyword list from day one. Search terms reviewed every 48 hours in the first two weeks
  • Bid strategy: Maximise conversions on Booked call, with a target CPA set after the first 15 to 20 bookings

Why: you want rapid results. Narrow, high intent queries with a matching page give you booked calls in days, without paying to train broad match.

4

Week 4: Remarketing and the first optimisation pass

  • Demand Gen and YouTube remarketing to page visitors: a 30 second case study video and a Hello Gordon demo clip
  • Customer Match from the CRM: exclude current clients, retarget no shows and open proposals
  • Test the Agency Switchers cluster on a comparison page
  • Move the best campaign to a qualified call goal once offline imports are flowing

Why: clinic owners rarely book on the first visit. Remarketing turns the clicks you already paid for into calls.

5

Ongoing: Reporting that matches your sales pipeline

  • Looker Studio dashboard: spend, cost per booked call, cost per qualified call, cost per closed plan, pipeline value
  • Weekly search term mining sent to your content team as AEO topic ideas, so paid search data feeds your organic engine
  • Phase 2 option: a packaged Google Ads add-on for your med spa clients, using the same tracking stack

Why: you want a long-term partner. This makes paid search part of the product, not a separate cost centre.
