1

Paid test audit on one account

Run your full Friday SOP, then a deeper pass on conversion actions, call settings, Landingi tags and the GHL lead source fields. You get a short summary of what was done, what was flagged and the two or three changes that would move CTR or cost per booking most.

Why: you see the work quality on a real account before committing to weekly support.
2

A lead reconciliation log for every client

One sheet, one row per client per Friday: Google Ads leads, Landingi submissions, GHL contacts and the gap. Anything over the agreed threshold goes to the Account Manager with the likely cause.

Why: a tracking break is caught in 7 days, not when a client questions the report.
3

Shared med spa negative keyword lists

Lists by theme, built from search terms across the roster: jobs and careers, training and certification, at-home and DIY, free and cheap, and treatments a clinic does not offer. Applied with the right match types, reviewed every Friday.

Why: one search term found in one account protects every account.
4

Feed booked patients back to Google Ads

On one pilot account, import booked and showed appointments from GHL as offline conversions, and set call conversions with a minimum length. Once proven, the same setup rolls to other clients.

Why: bidding learns from patients who book, which is what your reporting promises.
5

A Friday summary your team can scan in one minute

Per client in Basecamp: actions taken, flags with a suggested fix, and one optimization idea. Policy items for Botox, Dysport and PRP ads are listed on their own line.

Why: Account Managers get decisions, not a long report.
