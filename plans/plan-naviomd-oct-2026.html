1

Verify every pixel, CAPI and click ID end to end

Test clicks from each platform through the /go redirect to a paid order. Fix gaps with your developer using a written, step by step brief.

Why: no budget decision is safe until the data is.
2

Point bidding at paid patients

Set the paid order as the primary conversion on every platform, with intake start kept as a secondary signal for learning.

Why: platforms optimize toward whatever you tell them counts.
3

Clear policy risk before it costs delivery

Align product names across all pages, audit live ads against GLP-1 and telehealth rules, and work open disapprovals with platform support through to resolution.

Why: in this category, delivery limits cost more than a bad bid.
4

Restructure by product and intent

Separate weight loss, wellness and women's health so each has its own budget, creative and CPA target, then shift spend toward the cheapest paid patient.

Why: blended campaigns hide which product is actually profitable.
5

Launch a written testing roadmap

One variable per test, a minimum data threshold before calling a winner, and a weekly note explaining what changed and why.

Why: you get decisions you can follow, not a generic report.
