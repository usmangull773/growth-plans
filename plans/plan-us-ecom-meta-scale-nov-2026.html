01

What we found

Running several US brands from one team gives you speed, but it also means one weak signal or one payment problem can hold back every brand at once. Three areas decide how far spend can scale.

High impact

Scaling on a noisy signal

When Pixel and Conversions API events double count, miss value or send no margin context, Meta bids on purchase volume instead of profit. Spend rises, CPMs jump and ROAS drops the moment budgets move.

Typical multi-brand setupScale-ready setup
Pixel and CAPI purchases not deduplicatedOne event ID per order, deduplicated across browser and server
Low event match qualityHashed email, phone and external ID sent server side
Same ROAS target for every productSeparate campaigns and targets by margin tier
Refunds and chargebacks still counted as winsRefunded and disputed buyers excluded and fed back
High impact

Chargebacks put the payment rail and ad accounts at risk

Your hiring history shows chargeback work on a Shopify store. In DTC, disputes usually start in the ad: an offer or delivery promise the order cannot match. A high dispute rate threatens the payment processor, and poor buyer feedback raises Meta costs and can restrict ad accounts.

Where disputes startFix inside the Meta funnel
Ad implies fast shippingAd copy and landing page show real delivery windows
Buyer does not recognise the chargeBrand name matched across ad, store and payment descriptor
Long silence after purchaseTracking and order update flow triggered from day one
Retargeting past disputersRefund and dispute audiences excluded from every campaign
Medium impact

One operator, several brands, one system

With several brands, results depend on a repeatable system rather than effort. Each brand needs its own ad account, ROAS and CPA target and test calendar, under one naming convention and one weekly report.

The 30 day plan

Week 1

Week 2

Week 3
Creative and offer testing
Week 4
Controlled scaling
1

Clean Pixel and Conversions API on every store

Deduplicate purchase events, raise event match quality and send order value with margin context so bidding learns from profit.

2

Rebuild each brand by margin tier

High, mid and low margin products get their own campaigns and ROAS targets, so cheap products stop pulling bids down.

3

Run a weekly creative, audience and offer cycle

Three new concepts per brand each week (problem and solution, social proof, offer), with fatigued ads retired every 10 to 14 days.

4

Cut chargebacks at the ad level

Match delivery promises to real fulfilment, align brand names across ad and checkout, and exclude refund and dispute audiences.

5

Scale winners with hard rules

Raise budgets in steps of 15% or less, duplicate only proven ad sets and pause any step that pushes CPA past target.
