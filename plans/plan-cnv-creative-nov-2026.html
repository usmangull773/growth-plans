01

What we found

Your clients are judged on booked calls and new patients, but Google and Meta only optimize toward what they are told to count. Across dental, healthcare and local service accounts, the gap between "lead" and "patient" is where budget leaks. Three findings shape the plan.

1. Platforms are rewarded for leads, your clients pay for patients

High impact

When a form submit, a 10 second call or a Meta Instant Form counts as a conversion, Smart Bidding and Advantage+ go find more of those. Lead volume climbs while booked calls stay flat. Fixing this is a signal problem first, and a campaign problem second.

2. Meta's health and wellness restrictions limit bottom funnel signal for medical accounts

High impact

Since January 2025, Meta has limited lower funnel events for accounts it classifies as health and wellness. For dental and healthcare clients this means standard Lead and Schedule events can be restricted, and a browser pixel on medical pages carries HIPAA risk. The answer is a server side setup that sends neutral, PHI free signals.

1

Week 1: Tracking scorecard on every live account

Check GTM, GA4, CallRail, Meta pixel and CAPI, and offline imports on each account. Confirm which conversion is primary, whether calls have a minimum duration, whether duplicates are counted, and whether any PHI reaches the ad platforms. Each account gets a red, amber or green score.

Why: bidding is only as good as the signal it learns from.
2

Week 1 to 2: Rebuild conversions around booked calls

Primary conversions become qualified calls, booked appointments and qualified forms. Page views, clicks to call and short calls move to secondary. CallRail transcripts are scored with AI against each client's criteria (new patient, treatment interest, insurance fit), and only qualified calls are counted for bidding.

Why: this is the fastest way to close the gap between lead count and booked calls.
3

Week 2 to 3: Close the loop from the CRM to the ad platforms

Pipeline stages (qualified, booked, showed, signed or new patient) go back to Google Ads through offline conversion import and enhanced conversions for leads, and to Meta through Conversions API for CRM. On healthcare accounts this runs through your HIPAA attribution layer, so no PHI reaches either platform. Each stage gets a value, so bidding favors patients who show up.

Why: your clients judge success beyond the first call, so the platforms should too.
4

Week 2 to 4: Account fixes on Google and Meta

Google: AI assisted search term classification with human review, negatives shared across similar clients, Performance Max brand exclusions and search themes, location and schedule tightening, and Local Services Ads lead disputes on dental. Meta: higher intent Instant Forms with qualifying questions, compliant new patient offers within the health and wellness rules, creative refreshed before fatigue, and fast follow up so leads are reached while they are still warm.

Why: cuts junk leads at the source instead of filtering them afterwards.
5

Week 3 to 4: Reporting and a written change log

One Looker Studio template across clients: cost per booked call, cost per qualified lead, show rate and new patients, with lead count as a secondary line. Every change is logged with the reason, the expected effect and a review date, so any drop is caught and traced within days. AI drafts the weekly notes, and I check every number before it goes to a client.

Why: this matches your promise of a report on booked calls, patients and sales.

Google playbook for local practices

  • Search by treatment: implants, Invisalign, emergency, new patient. Each gets its own budget and cost per booked call ceiling.
  • Performance Max fed with offline booked patient values, brand excluded, search themes set.
  • Local Services Ads on dental and home services, with weekly lead disputes.
  • Call only and call assets during staffed hours, with a minimum call length counted.
  • B2B: offline import of SQL and closed deal stages, so bidding learns from pipeline.

Meta playbook for service and healthcare

  • Higher intent Instant Forms with qualifying questions and a review screen, which removes most junk leads.
  • Compliant new patient offers written within the health and wellness rules, no personal health claims.
  • Server side CAPI with neutral custom events and PHI scrubbed before anything leaves the site.
  • CRM stages back to Meta so delivery shifts toward people who book and show.
  • Event promotion with registration and attendance tracked as separate events.
