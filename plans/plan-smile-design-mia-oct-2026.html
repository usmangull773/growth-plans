03

BookedSmile: booking that reports back

BookedSmile is our own booking platform for dental practices. Patients book a consultation in three clicks, and every confirmed booking is sent server-side to Google Ads, GA4 and Meta, so your campaigns learn from real bookings instead of form fills.

Patients get email and WhatsApp confirmations and reminders, which is useful for Spanish-speaking patients, and every visit triggers an automatic Google review request to refresh your older reviews. It is set up for you in a day, and we run your campaigns on top of it.
