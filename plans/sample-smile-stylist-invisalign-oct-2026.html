Invisalign questions

Quick answers to what patients ask most. Your dentist will go through your own case at the free consultation.

How does Invisalign work?

Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners made from a digital scan of your teeth. Each set moves your teeth a small amount, and you change to the next set on a schedule set by your dentist.

How long does treatment take?

It varies by case. Minor corrections can take a few months, while more complex cases take longer. You will get an estimate for your own treatment at your consultation.

Does Invisalign hurt?

Most people feel some pressure or mild discomfort for a day or two when they start a new set of aligners. This is a sign the aligners are working and it usually settles quickly.

How often do I need to wear my aligners?

For the best results, aligners are usually worn about 20 to 22 hours a day. You take them out to eat, drink anything other than water, and brush your teeth.

Is finance available?

Yes. Interest free finance is available through Medenta Finance and V12 Finance, subject to status. The team can talk you through the options at your consultation.
