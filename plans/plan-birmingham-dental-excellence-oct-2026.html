Move online booking to BookedSmile so every confirmed booking reports back to Google Ads, GA4 and Meta

One booking route for the Discovery Day and every consultation, so patients book in three clicks and each confirmed booking is counted against the ad that produced it.

2

Google Search for dental implants, full arch and Invisalign in Birmingham

Campaigns built around the same offers already working on Meta: the Discovery Day, £1000 off full-arch treatment, the £1025 Invisalign saving and 0% finance, each with its own cost per booked consultation target.

Why: these are the patients already looking for the treatments you are known for.
3

Clean tracking: one GA4 property and Google Ads conversions on both sites

Move the main site and offers page onto one GA4 property, add the Google Ads conversion to the main site and the offers page, and remove the placeholder ID G-CCCHEXT123.

Why: one set of numbers means you can see which ad produced each booked consultation.
4

SEO: one location message, implant and Invisalign pages, Google Business Profile

Fort Dunlop as the practice location, serving Sutton Coldfield and Erdington, reflected in the title, H1, treatment pages and a Google Business Profile that matches them, so search and AI tools describe you correctly.

Why: organic enquiries cost nothing per click and keep coming when budgets change.
5

Follow-up automation for Discovery Day sign-ups and one weekly patient report

Instant reply, reminders and a rebook message for no-shows, then one report: searches, clicks, booked consultations and treatment started, by channel.

Why: with only 15 places, every sign-up that turns up matters, and you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
03

BookedSmile: booking that reports back

BookedSmile is our own booking platform for dental practices. Patients book in three clicks, and every confirmed booking is sent server-side to Google Ads, GA4 and Meta, so your campaigns learn from real patients.

Confirmations and reminders go out by email and WhatsApp, and every patient gets an automatic Google review request after the visit. We set it up for you in a day and run your campaigns on top of it.
