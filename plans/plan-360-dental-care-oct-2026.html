1

Feed real outcomes back to Google and Meta

Keep Smile Assessment submissions and portal bookings as conversions, then import consultation attended and treatment started from your practice system as offline conversions.

Why: bidding should learn from patients who start treatment, not from form fills alone.
2

Widen search coverage by treatment

Composite bonding, veneers, Invisalign, implants and facial aesthetics each get their own ad group, ad copy and cost per consultation target, so you show up where competitors run 19 to 69 ads.

Why: at £950 a veneer, the searches you are missing are the expensive ones.
3

Fast mobile landing pages for each treatment

One page per treatment with price, finance, before and after, reviews and the Smile Assessment above the fold, built to score 80 or higher on mobile.

Why: a slow first screen loses the enquiry before it starts.
4

Automate the follow-up

Instant reply to every enquiry, SMS and email reminders before consultations, a no-show rebook sequence, and a nudge for treatment plans not yet started. Membership patients get offered a cosmetic consultation.

Why: the cheapest new patient is the enquiry you already paid for.
5

One weekly report from enquiry to treatment

Enquiries, booked consultations, attended and treatment started, by treatment and by channel. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
