01

What we found

This review uses only public information: your website, your booking portal, Google Ads Transparency Center and Meta Ad Library. It is a starting point, not an account audit. What it shows clearly is that the booking step is where your tracking is weakest, and every bidding decision depends on that step.

Online bookings finish on a different domain with a different tag container

High impact

Your site loads Google Tag Manager GTM-PWXFBQLF and a Google Ads tag that records page views. Your online booking link sends patients to 360-dental-care.portal.dental, which loads a different container, GTM-KN9N27L. Unless that portal has been set up to send the booking back, Google Ads sees the click and the page view but not the booked consultation. Bidding then optimises toward visits and calls instead of patients.

The 30 day plan

1

Connect portal bookings to Google Ads and GA4

Pass the click ID from your site to the booking portal and send the confirmed booking back as the primary conversion. Calls and form fills stay as secondary signals.

Why: Google can only find more patients like the ones it is told about.
2

Install the Meta pixel with Conversions API

Add it through your existing GTM, then send Lead and Schedule events from both browser and server so Meta sees bookings even when cookies are blocked.

Why: your Meta ads are already spending without any outcome data.
3

Split campaigns by treatment

Composite bonding, veneers, Invisalign, implants and whitening each get their own ad group, landing page and cost per consultation target.

Why: blended campaigns hide which treatment is actually profitable.
4

Track calls by length and reconcile with the diary

Count a call as a lead only after a minimum duration, and check a weekly sample against booked consultations.

Why: a ringing phone is not a booked patient.
5

One weekly report from enquiry to treatment

Enquiries, booked consultations, attended and treatment started, by treatment and by channel. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
