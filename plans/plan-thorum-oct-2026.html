01

What we found

Thorum has what most jewelry brands pay agencies to invent: a real maker, real materials and thousands of happy customers. The gap is that the ads tell one version of that story to everyone, at the most expensive time of the year to run a single message.

High impact

One angle carries all 52 Meta ads

Every active ad we could see runs the same "Wear Your Story" headline and the same long body copy. When one message carries the whole account, frequency climbs and Meta has nothing new to learn from. A buyer looking for a gift for him, a groom looking for a meteorite band and a past customer all see the same thing.

High impact

Two different offers going into Black Friday

The ads promise 20% off with code Thorum20, while the homepage banner shows 25% off, Early Black Friday Sale. A shopper who clicks a 20% ad and lands on a 25% banner trusts it less, and the cheaper offer in the ad lowers click through before they arrive.

Medium impact

Google Search now runs under AI Max rules

Since 3 August, new campaign level broad match and legacy automatically created assets are closed, and from 1 September Google began moving eligible Search campaigns to AI Max. For a store where demand starts with a material, loose matching can pull budget into "meteorite" and "wood" searches that have nothing to do with rings, or pay for your own brand clicks twice.

1

Check every purchase signal and match the offer

Audit the Meta pixel and Conversions API, Google enhanced conversions and the GA4 purchase event against Shopify orders. Update every live ad to the current sale offer.

Why: both platforms bid on what they are told a sale is worth, so this has to be right before Black Friday traffic arrives.

2

Launch a Meta angle matrix

Six angles tested side by side: material origin, the maker in the workshop, gift for him, groom and wedding day, customer reviews and unboxing, and the sale offer. Built from your existing photo and video library first.

Why: the winning angle gets the budget, and Meta gets fresh signals instead of one tired message.

3

Rebuild Shopping and Performance Max by collection

Feed titles led by material and width, sale prices and promotions in Merchant Center, and best sellers such as The Aloha, Black Meteorite and The Thor split from the long tail.

Why: your top sellers get the spend they earn, and new designs still get tested.

4

Put guardrails on AI Max and brand traffic

Brand exclusions, a separate brand campaign, material negative lists and a weekly search terms review.

Why: AI Max can find good new searches, but only with limits set by someone who knows the product.

5

Scale through Black Friday and holiday gifting

Budgets raised on the angles and collections hitting target, retargeting built for cart and product viewers, shipping cutoff messaging added late in the season.

Why: the money goes where proof already exists, not where it is hoped for.
