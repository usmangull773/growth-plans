01

What we found

Your brief names the right outcomes: leads, qualified leads, CPL, payments and enrollments. The gap in most education accounts is that the ad platforms only see the first one, so they buy the cheapest form fill, not the most likely student.

High impact

The platforms are scored on leads, but you are paid on enrollments

When the only conversion event is a form submit, Meta, Snapchat and TikTok push budget toward people who fill forms easily. CPL looks healthy while qualified rate and payments stay flat. The fix is to send qualified, paid and enrolled events back to each platform so bidding learns from real students.

Optimizing for form submits
  • Cheap CPL, low intent
  • Sales team chases unqualified leads
  • No link between ad spend and payments
Optimizing for qualified and paid events
  • Bidding favors people who pay
  • Budget moves on cost per enrollment
  • Every riyal traced to a stage
Medium impact

Four platforms need four different jobs

Running Meta, Snapchat, TikTok and LinkedIn with the same goal and creative splits budget without a clear reason. Each channel should own a stage of the funnel and be judged on cost per qualified lead, not raw lead count.

PlatformRole in the funnelJudged on
MetaMain lead engine, instant forms and retargetingCost per qualified lead, cost per payment
SnapchatReach younger students and parents at scaleCost per qualified lead
TikTokVideo hooks that warm cold audiencesQualified rate, retargeting pool size
LinkedInProfessional and corporate programsCost per qualified lead
Medium impact

Creative and retargeting decide the cost per enrollment

Education buyers compare options for days before paying. Without a structured creative test and a retargeting layer for unpaid leads, warm prospects drop out and you pay to acquire them again. A weekly test matrix plus stage-based retargeting fixes this.

TestVariantsDecision rule
HookCareer outcome, price, certificate, testimonialKeep the lowest cost per qualified lead
FormatShort video, static, carouselScale the winner, pause after 48 hrs if behind
RetargetingVideo viewers, form openers, unpaid leadsJudge on cost per payment

1

Map the funnel and send stages back to the platforms

Lead, qualified, payment and enrollment events go back through Meta Conversions API and each platform's event tools, so bidding learns from students who pay.

2

Restructure campaigns by program and funnel stage

Each program gets its own budget and cost per qualified lead ceiling, so one weak course cannot drain the account.

3

Run a weekly Arabic creative test matrix

Three to four hooks per ad set, judged on qualified rate, so creative decisions come from data rather than taste.

4

Build retargeting for video viewers and unpaid leads

Warm prospects are the cheapest payments in the account, and most of them need more than one touch.

5

Move budget daily toward the lowest cost per enrollment

Winners scale in controlled steps, losers stop within 48 hours, and every change is written in the daily update.
