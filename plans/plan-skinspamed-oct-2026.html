1

Online booking requests with an instant reply, and measurement that reports back

Connect every booking and consultation outcome back to Google Ads and Meta on your existing booking system. That means adding online booking requests with an instant reply, or call tracking on the phone line, so each appointment is counted against the ad or search that produced it.

Why: bidding should learn from booked patients, not from clicks.
2

Google Search for Ultherapy, CO2 and Botox in Dallas and Colleyville

Campaigns for your highest value treatments, with your 20+ years, 4.9 rating and Medical Director in the ad copy, each sent to its own treatment page with a cost per booked appointment target.

Why: these are the patients already searching for what you do, and today no paid result from you appears.
3

Meta and Instagram for memberships, plus retargeting

Campaigns built around your monthly memberships of 2 to 4 treatments, plus retargeting for people who visited the site but did not book, using the Meta Pixel you already have.

Why: aesthetics patients compare for weeks, and the med spa they keep seeing gets the booking.
4

SEO and Google Business Profile for both locations

Separate Dallas and Colleyville pages and profiles, updated prices on treatment pages, and the site details fixed: newsletter block, location wording and the CoolSculpting URL.

Why: organic inquiries cost nothing per click and keep coming when budgets change.
5

Follow-up automation and one weekly patient report

Reminders, a rebook message when a treatment is due and membership follow-up, then one report: searches, clicks, booked appointments and memberships started, by channel.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
