01

What we found

Your ads are not the problem. The problem is that the data trail breaks in three places between the click and the payment, so neither Meta nor Google ever learns which leads turned into patients.

Intake is split across several workflows, so leads go missing

High impact

Each Meta instant form feeds its own GHL intake workflow. When one of them misses a step, the contact lands with no tag, no owner and no opportunity. Those leads never reach a pipeline, so they never count as a held call or a close.

Google only sees held calls from some calendars

High impact

The offline "Held Call" conversion fires from only part of your calendars, and CF7 leads arrive with no GCLID. Google is bidding on a partial picture of your real results, which pushes budget toward the wrong searches.

1

Map every entry point, workflow and conversion action

Each Meta form, Google campaign, CF7 form and calendar traced to its GHL workflow, pipeline stage, pixel event and Google conversion action. Delivered as a prioritized fix list and a short walkthrough call with you.

Why: nothing gets changed until we know which break costs the most data.

2

Route all intake through one router workflow

One intake workflow handles tags, owner assignment and opportunity creation for every source. The old form workflows are cloned, tested on test contacts, then retired without stopping live leads.

Why: one path means no orphaned leads and one place to maintain.

3

Capture UTM, GCLID and fbclid on every entry point

GTM stores click IDs and UTMs on landing, CF7 passes them through hidden fields, and GHL writes them to contact fields. Meta lead forms carry dynamic campaign and ad values instead of static labels.

Why: a conversion can only be sent back if the click that started it was kept.

4

Send held calls and closes back to Google and Meta

One "appointment showed" trigger covers every calendar and sends a Held Call to Google Ads offline conversion import and to Meta CAPI. Won stages send the close with its value. Enhanced conversions for leads catch the contacts that arrive without a GCLID.

Why: both platforms start optimizing toward patients who show up, not toward cheap form fills.

5

Connect Stripe and Monday.com, then report and document

Stripe payments and Monday.com status changes move the GHL opportunity to Won by webhook. A Looker Studio view shows cost per lead, cost per held call and cost per close by campaign and ad, with written documentation for your team.

Why: you see spend to revenue in one place, and your GHL builder can maintain it.
