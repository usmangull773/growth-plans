01

What we found

This review is based on your public site, pricing and job brief. The account itself still needs to be checked, but the pattern is clear. DCatalog sells two products at very different price points, and Google cannot tell them apart unless the account tells it. Every finding below comes back to that.

One account, two very different buyers

High impact

The Select plan starts at $29 a month. Catalog Automation starts at $7,000 a year. Flipbook keywords bring in the first group, along with students and people who want a free tool. Automation and B2B catalog keywords bring in the second. When both count as a lead, bidding drifts toward whichever is cheaper to get, which is almost always the free flipbook search.

Low value intent

  • free flipbook maker
  • pdf to flipbook free / online
  • flipbook for school project
  • free catalog template download
  • how to make a flipbook
Fits a $29 plan at best

Demo worthy intent

  • catalog automation software
  • generate catalog from PIM / ERP
  • distributor and dealer catalog platform
  • price list and line sheet software
  • B2B digital catalog with quote requests
Fits a $7,000+ annual contract

The fix is a search term review sorted by these two groups, a shared negative list applied to Search and Performance Max, and budget weighted toward the groups that produce qualified leads in Zoho.

Two demo pages, two different messages

High impact

Your site runs two demo routes. The Catalog Automation page sends visitors to a demo page that shows "Pricing starts at $7,000 / Annually" above the form. That one line filters out people who are not ready for that budget. The general Request a Demo page has no price signal, and its search snippet promotes the 7 day free trial with no credit card.

Performance Max and Demand Gen need guardrails

Medium impact

Both campaign types find the cheapest conversion available. In a SaaS account that has a free trial, that usually means trial sign-ups and brand searches that would have come in anyway. These are the controls that keep them pointed at demos.

1
Week 1

Search term audit and a shared negative list

Pull 90 days of search terms from Search and Performance Max. Tag every term as automation, B2B catalog, flipbook, free or student, competitor, or brand. Build one shared negative list from the free, student and one-off groups and apply it across the account. Turn off auto-applied recommendations.

Why: this is the fastest way to stop paying for clicks that cannot become a qualified demo.

2
Week 1 to 2

Match campaigns to Zoho outcomes

Use the Zoho import to see qualified leads by campaign, ad group and keyword, not just demo requests. Rank each one by cost per qualified lead.

Why: a pricier demo that qualifies costs less than a cheap demo that never turns into a deal. Budget should follow cost per qualified lead.

3
Week 2

Split Search by intent and move budget

Separate catalog automation, B2B and distributor catalogs, flipbook (Elite tier), and brand into their own campaigns or ad groups with their own budgets. Shift spend toward the groups with the lowest cost per qualified lead.

Why: once each intent has its own budget, flipbook clicks can no longer take money from automation demos.

4
Week 2 to 4

Ad copy that qualifies, and the right demo page

Test automation ads that name the buyer (manufacturers, distributors) and the system (PIM, ERP, CSV), with one variant that says "plans from $7,000 a year". Send those ad groups to the priced demo page. Suggest landing page changes based on what the data shows.

Why: ads and pages that state the price filter out low budget clicks before you pay for them.

5
Week 3 to 4

Guardrails on Performance Max and Demand Gen, then the change list

Add brand exclusions, B2B search themes and customer list exclusions to Performance Max. Seed Demand Gen audiences from qualified Zoho leads. In week 4, deliver the prioritized change list, with any conversion or bidding proposals listed separately for your approval.

Why: automated campaigns perform well once they are told which leads count. Without that, they go after volume.
