01

What we found

In a booked-call business the ad platform only sees the top of the funnel. The cheapest lead is often the most expensive client, because the drop-off happens after the opt-in, where Meta cannot see it.

The engine is already built across AdvisorOS, InovaOS and LaunchOS: VSL funnels, setter and closer pods, show-up optimisation, Hyros attribution and Looker Studio reporting. What it needs from paid media is a buyer who reads the numbers back from the sales side every day. The person changing the ads should be the person reading what they closed, so a cheap lead that never shows gets caught in days, not at month end.

The cheapest CPL ad set can be the costliest per held call

High impact

An ad set that wins on cost per lead but books and shows less than the rest is usually buying a different audience: lower intent, wrong market, or people attracted by a hook that does not match the offer. Meta keeps feeding it budget because it is optimising for the opt-in. The fix is to judge it on cost per held call and cost per closed client, then cut, re-target or rebuild it based on which step it leaks at. For offers sold to financial advisers or technical consultants, the application answers and setter notes usually show the reason within the first week of data.

The leak needs to be found by funnel step, not by platform

High impact

Each step has its own conversion rate, and each ad set has its own pattern. Reading them side by side shows exactly which step is leaking, so the action is specific: change the audience, the creative, the page, the application or the booking flow.

Meta, Hyros and the CRM will never show the same number

Medium impact

Meta counts view-through and modelled conversions, Hyros applies its own attribution windows and first or last click, and the CRM only counts what sales logged. Matching all three on ad ID, UTM and email shows where the platform is over-reporting and which ads truly produce revenue. The same joined table can feed your Looker Studio views, so the dashboard and the buying decisions run off one source.

1
Week 1

Tracking QA on every account

Check pixels, CAPI event match quality, UTMs on every live ad and offline conversion uploads. Confirm Hyros is receiving the ad ID for each lead.

Reason: if a signal is broken, every decision after it is wrong.

2
Weeks 1 to 2

Build the spend-to-revenue reconciliation

One sheet per account joining Ads Manager, Hyros and CRM data, with lookups and pivots for cost per booked call, cost per held call and cost per closed client, first click and last click.

Reason: it shows which ads produce clients, not just leads.

3
Week 2

Funnel leak review per ad set

Compare step rates for every ad set against the account average and flag the step that drops off. The cheapest CPL ad set gets read the same way: book rate, show rate, close rate, lead source and qualification answers.

Reason: the leaking step tells you whether to cut, re-target or rebuild.

4
Weeks 2 to 4

Point optimisation at booked and held calls

Send booked call and held call events back to Meta through CAPI and to Google as offline conversions, then test campaigns optimising for those events against the lead event.

Reason: the algorithm finds more of whatever event it is fed.

5
Weeks 3 to 4

Creative tests with clear kill rules

Weekly creative launches, losers cut on cost per held call, winners scaled in steps. Short written updates: what changed, why, and what was done.

Reason: fast cuts protect budget for the ads that close.
