01

What we found

Navira has spent more than 10 years mastering Amazon: Buy Box control, MAP protection, catalog and logistics. The Meta role is a different muscle. The brands sell on Shopify and Amazon at the same time, and Navira earns on the retail margin. That means the wrong scorecard can make a profitable Meta campaign look like a loser, or the reverse. Three areas decide whether Meta scales profitably.

High impact

Meta drives Amazon sales that Meta never sees

A shopper sees a Reel, then searches the brand on Amazon and buys there. Ads Manager records no purchase, the ad set gets cut, and the Amazon lift disappears with it. For an Amazon-first portfolio, in-platform ROAS undercounts what Meta is doing.

Meta adto Shopify storeor Amazon searchto Sale Meta cannot attribute
Meta adto Amazon Attribution tagto Sale tracked, Brand Referral Bonus where eligible

The fix is a blended view: Shopify MER, Amazon sales from tagged Meta traffic, and a holdout test before any big budget call.

High impact

One ROAS target cannot fit every brand

Navira buys at wholesale and earns in the margin, so every SKU has its own profit line. A 2.0 ROAS can be strong on a high-margin beauty item and a loss on a low-margin accessory. Targets must come from contribution margin, product by product.

Common multi-brand setupMargin-led setup
Same ROAS goal across all brandsBreakeven and target ROAS per SKU from landed cost, fees and shipping
Pixel and Conversions API purchases double countedOne event ID per order, deduplicated, high match quality
Budget moves on 3-day ROAS swingsDecisions on 7-day MER, CAC and new-customer share
Bundles and best sellers treated alikeHigher AOV bundles pushed to cold traffic to protect margin
Medium impact

Creative volume is the real bottleneck

On Meta, the creative is the targeting. Across several brands, results depend on a steady flow of new concepts, designed, tested and documented by the same person who reads the data, so nothing is lost between a designer and a media buyer.

The 30 day plan

Weeks 1 to 4

Week 1
Audit and tracking
Week 2
Structure and creative sprint
Week 3
Testing cycle
Week 4
Scale winners
1

Audit every account and fix the signal

Review spend, ROAS, CPA and frequency by brand. Check Pixel and Conversions API deduplication, event match quality and Shopify purchase values. Reason: Meta can only optimise as well as the data it receives.

2

Build a margin sheet per brand

Breakeven ROAS, target CAC and AOV for each SKU, plus a blended MER goal per brand. Reason: every budget decision after this point is checked against profit, not a single platform number.

3

Restructure into testing, scaling and retargeting

One clean testing campaign, one scaling campaign for proven ads, one retargeting layer, with the same naming convention across all brands. Reason: faster reading of results and fewer resets in delivery.

4

Ship the first creative sprint

New static, carousel and short video concepts per brand, built from reviews, competitor ads and best sellers. Reason: fresh creative is the main lever for lower CPA on Meta.

5

Tag Amazon traffic and scale what wins

Amazon Attribution links on Amazon-led products, budget steps of 15 to 20 percent on winners, and a weekly report with insights and next actions. Reason: Meta gets credit for the Amazon sales it drives, and scaling stays controlled.
