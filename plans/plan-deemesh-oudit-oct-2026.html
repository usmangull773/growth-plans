1

Track every enquiry as a real conversion

Form submissions, phone taps and calls from the site recorded in GA4 and Google Ads, tagged by procedure. Then consultation booked and surgery confirmed imported from your practice system.

Why: bidding should learn from patients who go ahead, not from form fills alone.
2

One clear consultation offer and a shorter form

Agree one message (free or paid, and with whom), cut the form to name, phone, procedure and preferred time, and state when the team will reply. Add a call button that works on mobile.

Why: a patient who has to stop and think at the form often books on a directory instead.
3

Launch search campaigns for your highest value procedures

Breast, tummy tuck, face and neck lift, and Vaser liposuction each get their own ad group, Manchester and North West targeting, and a cost per consultation target. Your name and My Cosmetic Clinic are protected in brand search.

Why: at £5,500 and above per procedure, a small number of extra consultations pays for the test.
4

Fix the on page signals Google reads first

A specific main heading on each key page, unique meta descriptions, consistent use of "Mr", prices shown on procedure pages, British locale settings and an llms.txt file so AI search can describe your practice correctly.

Why: clear signals help both organic rankings and ad quality, lowering cost per click.
5

One weekly report from search to consultation

Enquiries, booked consultations, attended and surgery confirmed, by procedure and by channel. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions in five minutes, not a longer dashboard.
