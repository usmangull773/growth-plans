01 What we found

The work is not the problem. The handoffs are.

Your brief and your recent hiring history point to the same thing: good specialists, but each piece of delivery lives with a different person. With 15+ accounts, that is where launches slip, tracking drifts and reports get rebuilt by hand. The fix is to put every channel behind one lead and one QA process.

Finding 1: Delivery is split across separate hires

High impact

Website builds, Google Ads help, conversion tracking, data lists and a Meta audit were each handled by a different freelancer. Every handoff costs you time in briefing and checking. One pod with shared SOPs removes that cost.

One delivery pod
  • One lead who owns every task and deadline
  • One Slack channel per client, under your brand
  • One written SOP library you keep
  • One QA gate before anything goes live

Finding 2: Tracking is what proves your results

High impact

Your agency sells on tracked revenue, not clicks. Across 15 accounts on Meta, GTM, HighLevel and different CRMs, one weak pixel or one untested lead form means a client sees fewer results than you actually produced. Every account gets the same tracking standard.

Without a standardWith the standard
Pixel only, browser events lost to blockers and iOSPixel plus Conversions API with matching event IDs, so nothing is counted twice
Lead forms trusted, not testedA test lead pushed through every form into HighLevel or the CRM before each launch
A different GTM setup on every client siteOne GTM container template and one naming convention for all accounts
Event and ticket sales counted in-platform onlyPurchase value passed back so ROAS matches the ticketing or Shopify numbers

Finding 3: Recurring work needs a fixed calendar

Medium impact

Weekly updates, monthly decks, GBP posts and blogs come back every month for every client. Putting each one on a fixed day stops it competing with launches for attention.

Week 1

Access, audit and a health score for every account

  • Partner access to each Business Manager, GTM, HighLevel and site, plus your Slack and SOP folder
  • Audit of pixel, Conversions API, event match quality, lead forms and CRM sync on all 15+ accounts
  • A test lead through every live form, with any break fixed the same day

Why: you see the exact state of every account before anything is touched.
2
Week 2

Full Meta takeover under your brand

  • Daily launches, testing and optimization across all accounts
  • One naming convention and one testing cadence: hooks, offers, audiences, lead form questions
  • First creative batch: statics and short video with copy, built in Canva and Figma
  • Event and ticketing accounts get countdown and retargeting structures for each date
Why: Meta is 60% of the work, so it moves first and moves cleanly.
3
Week 3

SEO, GBP, web and basic Search join the system

  • Monthly GBP post calendar and blog plan for each local client
  • On-page fixes listed and worked in priority order
  • WordPress, Wix and Shopify edits, landing pages and HighLevel hookups through one request queue
  • Basic Search campaigns built or cleaned up where clients have them
Why: local clients get found on maps and search, not only in the feed.
4
Week 3 to 4

Reporting on a fixed calendar

  • Weekly update inputs every Friday, written for you to forward as is
  • Monthly report decks by the 3rd business day, built from AgencyAnalytics or your template
  • CPL, cost per booked lead and ROAS shown next to the client's own sales numbers
Why: you spend minutes reviewing reports, not hours building them.
5
Week 4

QA review and the scale plan for month two

  • Review of every launch against the QA checklist, with misses written into the SOPs
  • Per account plan: what to scale, what to cut, what creative to brief next
  • TikTok Ads test proposal for accounts where it fits
Why: month two starts with a plan, not a catch-up.
