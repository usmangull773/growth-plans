1

Track every enquiry through to consultation booked

Form submissions on the thank-you page, calls to 0161 236 6500 and clicks to call as conversions, then consultation booked and attended imported from your clinic system as offline conversions.

Why: Google should learn from patients who pay for a consultation, not from form fills alone.
2

Google Search campaigns by procedure

Breast enlargement, breast uplift, tummy tuck, mummy makeover, facelift and blepharoplasty each get their own ad group, ad copy and cost per consultation target, across Manchester, Cheshire and Lancashire. Brand search protected from day one.

Why: at £6,250 to £11,500 a procedure, the searches you are missing are the valuable ones.
3

Consultation landing pages

One focused page per key procedure: guide price, the £240 consultation deducted from surgery, awards, before and after results, named reviews and a short form above the fold, with the phone number one tap away.

Why: a patient ready to book should not have to pick from 19 options.
4

Automate the follow-up

Instant reply to every enquiry with next steps and the consultation fee, a same day call task for the team, reminders before consultations, and a gentle sequence for patients who were quoted but have not booked surgery.

Why: the cheapest new patient is the enquiry you already paid for.
5

SEO, AEO and GEO fixes

Rewrite the homepage heading, add missing meta descriptions, publish an llms.txt file, build a gynaecomastia page, fix the clinics link and refresh the main procedure pages with current prices and FAQs so search and AI tools can quote them.

Why: patients now ask Google and AI tools who to see, and the answer comes from clear pages.
6

One weekly report from search to consultation

Enquiries, consultations booked, attended and surgery booked, by procedure and by channel. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
