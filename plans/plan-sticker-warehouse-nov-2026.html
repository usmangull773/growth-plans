01

What we found

This review uses only your public storefront and product data. It points to one root cause: the feed is built from a store that was designed for humans configuring a custom order, not for Google reading a catalogue. Sizes and quantities live in thousands of variants, and none of them carry the structure Merchant Centre needs to tell them apart.

1. A small catalogue becomes a very large feed

High impact

Every Size and Quantity combination is a separate Shopify variant, and Simprosys sends each variant to Merchant Centre as its own item. 46 products turn into 2,642 items. One product can range from a small trial order to a bulk run costing over 60 times more, all under almost the same title.

2. The fields Google and your ad manager need are empty

Medium impact

None of the 2,642 variants has a SKU, and the product type field is blank on all 46 products. The Instant Sticker Builder variants carry app-generated hash names, and 13 Trade listings sit at a flat $50. Without these fields, custom labels and campaign splits have nothing to read.

Feed today

  • Product type blank on 46 of 46
  • SKU blank on 2,642 of 2,642
  • Builder variants named like fbcdd7bc4d84...
  • Trade and retail items mixed together
  • Titles do not state size, quantity or finish

Feed after week 2

  • Product type mapped per range: standard, specialty, roll labels, magnets
  • identifier_exists = no set for custom printed items
  • App-generated and Trade items excluded or labelled on purpose
  • Five custom labels the ad manager can bid on
  • Titles carrying material, size, quantity and finish

3. Discounts need to reach Google the same way the customer sees them

High impact

Six products currently show compare-at pricing on site. Google only accepts a sale price it can verify on the landing page at crawl time. Discounts applied through codes, automatic discounts or bonus-sticker offers do not travel through the feed on their own. They need either a correctly dated sale_price or a Merchant Centre promotion. Otherwise items risk price mismatch warnings right when Black Friday traffic peaks.

The 30 day plan

Compliance and discounts come first because of Black Friday. Structure and titles follow, and then the feed moves to a fixed weekly update routine run with your advertising account manager in Slack.

Ongoing
1

Merchant Centre compliance audit

  • Diagnostics, item disapprovals and account-level warnings
  • Shipping settings matched to free express shipping Australia wide and 1 to 2 day handling
  • Return policy, business details and checkout checks

Why: account-level issues hide every item at once, so they get cleared before any optimisation.

2

Discount handling before Black Friday

  • Compare-at items sent as price plus sale_price with effective dates
  • Code-based and automatic discounts set up as Merchant Centre promotions
  • Spot checks of landing page price against feed price on the sale items

Why: a mismatched sale price triggers warnings exactly when the account can least afford downtime.

3

Simprosys feed rules and exclusions

  • Product type and Google product category mapped by range
  • identifier_exists = no for custom printed products
  • App-generated builder variants and Trade listings excluded or labelled by agreement

Why: rules in Simprosys survive Shopify edits, so the weekly update never undoes the fix.

4

Title and description rewrite, agreed with the ad manager

  • Formula: Custom {Finish} {Shape} Stickers, {Size}, {Quantity} Pack, Printed in Australia
  • Descriptions lead with material, laminate and turnaround

Why: shoppers search "custom holographic stickers 7.5cm", so the title has to say it.

5

Custom labels and the weekly update cycle

  • Label 0 range, Label 1 quantity tier, Label 2 price band, Label 3 promotion, Label 4 bestseller
  • Every week: diagnostics, price and sale sync, new products, a short change log in Slack

Why: labels let Shopping campaigns bid differently on trial orders and bulk runs.
