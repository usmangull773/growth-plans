What we found

I reviewed borderwisetax.com, the Google listing, the Meta Ad Library and the Google index on 17 November 2026. The expertise is clearly there. The gap is that nothing on the site can tell an ad platform which enquiry became a paying client, so every campaign would start blind.

1. There is no measurement layer, so ads cannot optimize toward qualified leads

High impact

A scan of the live homepage found no GA4, no Google Tag Manager, no Meta Pixel and no Google Ads conversion tag, and no structured data. Your job post asks for cost per qualified lead and consultation bookings. Neither can be reported until these are in place.

2. The landing experience asks a cold visitor to write an email, not book a consultation

High impact

The homepage sells four practice areas at once: U.S. tax, Canadian tax, ERP and AI agents. The contact page is a general form (Name, Email, Subject, Question, plus an "Email To" field) with no booking step and no qualifying questions. The site does not show the credentials that make you the right choice (CPA, USCPA, IRS Certifying Acceptance Agent). The phone number on the site (506 705 2885) also differs from the Google listing (506 705 1728).

Today

  • Ads would land on a broad page covering tax, ERP and AI
  • Open text form, so every enquiry needs manual triage
  • No calendar, so a hot lead waits for an email reply
  • No credentials, office address or reviews near the form
  • Two different phone numbers across site and Google

After week 2

  • One landing page per cross-border situation
  • Form asks residence, situation, filings needed and deadline
  • Qualified leads go straight to a consultation booking step
  • Credentials, Richmond Hill office and 5.0 Google rating shown
  • One phone number everywhere, tracked as a conversion

The 30 day plan

Measurement first, then landing pages, then spend. Budget only scales once we can see cost per qualified lead by campaign.

1

Install the measurement layer on Odoo (week 1)

GTM, GA4, Google Ads conversions, Meta Pixel and Conversions API. Events: form submitted, consultation booked, phone click, WhatsApp click and secure file upload. Then connect the Odoo CRM stages so a lead marked "Qualified" or "Engaged" is sent back to Google Ads and Meta as an offline conversion.

Why: both platforms then bid for people who become clients, not for anyone who fills a form.

2

Build cross-border landing pages with a qualifying form (weeks 1 to 2)

Three focused pages: Canadians with U.S. income or a U.S. LLC, U.S. citizens living in Canada, and people moving between the two countries. Each one shows credentials, the Richmond Hill office and the Google rating, plus a short form (country of residence, situation, filings needed, deadline) that leads to a consultation booking step.

Why: qualifying questions filter out low-value enquiries before they cost your team time.

3

Launch Google Search on high-intent cross-border terms (weeks 2 to 4)

Tight ad groups by situation: cross-border tax accountant in Toronto and the GTA, U.S. tax return for Canadian residents, FBAR and T1135 filing, ITIN through a Certifying Acceptance Agent, U.S. LLC owned by a Canadian, and Form 5471. Exact and phrase match, with a negative list for DIY software, free calculators, jobs and courses from day one.

Why: search catches people with a filing problem and a deadline, and those leads are the most likely to book.

4

Run Facebook and Instagram in two layers (weeks 3 to 4)

Layer one retargets site and blog visitors with consultation offers. Layer two runs prospecting lead ads built on your strongest topics (TN visa, U.S. LLC, moving between countries), using higher-intent lead forms with the same qualifying questions. Two to three ad concepts per audience, each tested against cost per qualified lead.

Why: Meta is cheaper for reach, and qualifying questions plus CAPI feedback keep lead quality close to search.

5

Weekly report on spend, qualified leads and bookings (week 4 onward)

A simple dashboard showing spend, cost per lead, cost per qualified lead and consultations booked, split by campaign, keyword theme and audience. Budget moves every week toward whatever produces booked consultations at the lowest cost.

Why: you see exactly what each dollar produced, in the terms your firm cares about.
