The 30 day plan

Built around the nine points in your post: Meta setup, GHL, AI and prompting, QA, reporting and working through the account manager.

Week 1
Audit and QA checklist
Week 2
GHL lead routing tests
Week 3
Optimise live campaigns
Week 4
AI prompts and reporting
1

Audit every live client account and write the QA checklist

  • Objective, audience, placements, budget, schedule and optimisation event per ad set
  • Pixel and CAPI events checked in Events Manager, one owner per event
  • A pre launch and post launch checklist your account managers can sign off

Why: one written standard means no campaign goes live on memory alone.

2

Test every lead path inside LeadNerds (GHL)

  • Submit a test lead through each funnel, form, calendar and Meta instant form
  • Confirm pipeline stage, tags, owner assignment, notifications and SMS or email follow up
  • Log each test with a screenshot so the client record shows it was checked

Why: the CRM is where a paid lead becomes a booked client, so it is tested like the ads are.

3

Optimise against cost per lead, not clicks

  • Weekly creative and audience tests per client, one variable at a time
  • Budget moved toward ad sets holding the client's cost per lead target
  • Rejected ads and delivery issues fixed the same day and documented

Why: your sales page sells a cost per lead, so that is the number every change is judged on.

4

Build a shared AI prompt library

  • Prompts for audience research, ad copy variations within Meta policy, hook ideas for video and multicultural angles
  • A prompt that turns raw Ads Manager exports into a plain English weekly summary
  • Every AI output checked against brand guidelines and policy before use

Why: the team gets faster without lowering accuracy, and the prompts stay with the agency.

5

Weekly reporting through the account manager

  • One page per client: spend, leads, cost per lead, what changed and what is next
  • Issues and budget risks flagged before the client meeting, not during it

Why: the account manager walks into every call with answers, and the client sees insight, not just numbers.
