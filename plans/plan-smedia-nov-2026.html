01 What we found

The bottleneck is not strategy. It is execution volume.

Your strategists, blueprints and automation are already in place. The role exists because ticket volume across hundreds of dealer accounts has to clear inside 24 hours without errors, and every mistake costs a dealer the metric you promised them.

Every ticket touches a live inventory feed on one of 7 formats

High impact

Your paid ads model syncs dealer inventory into live feeds and sends shoppers straight to VDPs. A budget change, a creative swap or a geo edit lands on one of seven different formats, each with its own rules. Speed without a per-format check is where QA failures come from.

Tracking is the product you sell, so a missing tag is a broken promise

High impact

sMedia optimises to three conversion metrics: Engaged Prospect Metric (30 seconds or more on a VDP), click-to-call and completed forms, on a clean GA4 instance per dealer. A launch that goes live without the right pixel or event means the dealer sees spend with no proof.

Launch without a tag check

  • Pixel fires on page view only
  • EPM and calls not recorded in GA4
  • Bidding learns from the wrong signal
  • Dealer report shows clicks, no outcomes

Launch with the blueprint check

  • EPM, call and form events verified in GTM preview
  • GA4 DebugView and platform event test confirmed
  • Conversion goal set as primary before spend
  • Ticket closed with screenshot proof

Daily pacing across many accounts needs one view, not one tab per dealer

Medium impact

Dealer budgets often include OEM co-op money and month-end targets. Checking every account by hand is slow, so a shared pacing sheet with simple thresholds makes the daily check fast and turns exceptions into tickets.

02 The 30 day plan

From onboarding to owning the queue in four weeks

The goal is simple: hit the 98% SLA and 95% first-pass QA targets by week four, then report on them every week.

Week by week

Week 1
Access and blueprints
Week 2
Tickets with QA checklist
Week 3
Pacing and tag routine
Week 4
Full queue and SLA report
1

Learn the blueprints and tools before touching live accounts

Access to the manager accounts, Fluency, the ticket system and blueprint library. Shadow 10 to 15 completed tickets to learn your naming, structure and QA standard.

Why: first-pass QA starts with doing it your way, not my way.
2

Build a one page QA checklist per platform

Seven short checklists, one per format in the table above, attached to every ticket. Each closed ticket includes a screenshot of the change and the check.

Why: errors come from skipped steps, and a checklist removes them.
3

Work the queue in priority order inside CST hours

Launches and stalled spend first, then budget edits, then copy and creative swaps. Same-day acknowledgement on every ticket, with blockers raised to the strategist the moment they appear.

Why: the 24 hour SLA is won by clearing the urgent work early in the day.
4

Run a daily pacing and tag health check

One pacing sheet across assigned accounts with the four status rules, plus a weekly tag spot check on new launches using GTM preview and GA4 DebugView.

Why: problems caught in the morning never become a dealer complaint.
5

Report the KPIs every Friday

Tickets closed, percentage inside SLA, first-pass QA rate, average completion time, and any account that needs a strategist decision.

Why: you see the work against your own KPIs without asking.
