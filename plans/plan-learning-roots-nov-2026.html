What we found

A quick outside look at learningroots.com shows a well built Shopify store with the right platforms installed. The gap is not the tools, it is how much the ad platforms can trust the purchase data they are bidding on. Fix that, and scaling becomes a numbers decision instead of a guess.

High impact

The Meta connection appears to be running on browser data only

The Facebook pixel is installed through the Shopify app, but the configuration shows no server side (Conversions API) token. Since iOS 14, browser only tracking misses a share of purchases, so Meta under reports sales and optimises on a thinner signal.

High impact

Two analytics sources can count the same order twice

The Google and YouTube app sends GA4 and Google Ads events, and a second app also sends analytics events with its own measurement setup. If both report purchases into the same GA4 property, revenue and ROAS look better than they are, and budget gets scaled on the wrong number.

Medium impact

The product feed is not ready for structured Shopping and Performance Max

In the public catalogue, 27 of 81 products have no product type, so Google cannot split campaigns by books, games, flashcards and courses. Bundles and free downloads are also in the catalogue and need different treatment.

1

Week 1: verify every purchase event

Connect Meta Conversions API through Shopify, test event match quality, and confirm one purchase per order across Meta, GA4 and Google Ads. Bidding is only as good as the signal it reads.

2

Week 1 to 2: one source of truth for revenue

Pick a single GA4 purchase source, switch off the duplicate, and set Google Ads to the Shopify purchase action as primary. Reported ROAS then matches the Shopify order report.

3

Week 2 to 3: feed and Google structure

Add product types and custom labels for age, category and margin, then split Shopping and Performance Max by product group with brand Search kept separate. Budget follows the groups that convert.

4

Week 2 to 4: Meta funnel by intent

Prospecting on Advantage+ with parent and age based angles, retargeting for viewers and cart abandoners, and a free download audience moved toward first purchase. Each stage gets its own CPA target.

5

Week 3 to 4: bundles, testing and staged scaling

Push bundles that clear the £50 free delivery line, test three creative angles a week, and raise budget in 20% steps only where ROAS holds, building toward the Ramadan 2027 peak.
