01

What we found

From your brief: results have flattened, there are too many junk calls and too much spend outside the area. In local service accounts these two problems almost always share a cause. Google is being told that every call is a win, and it is allowed to show ads to people who are not in Dallas.

HIGH IMPACT

The location setting is letting ads leave the service area

The default option targets people "in, or interested in" the location. Someone in Houston or out of state who searches about Dallas can see and click the ad. Broad radius targeting and Search Partners make it worse.

Default setup

  • Presence or interest targeting
  • One wide radius around the city
  • Search Partners and Display left on
  • No location exclusions

Fixed setup

  • Presence only: people in or regularly in the area
  • Service ZIP codes or tight radius per zone
  • Search Partners and Display off
  • Out of area cities and states excluded
HIGH IMPACT

Junk calls are counted as conversions, so bidding chases more of them

If every call counts, a 9 second wrong number looks the same as a booked job. Smart bidding then optimizes toward cheap, short, low intent calls, which is exactly why lead quality drops while volume looks fine.

1

Full account audit, delivered to your agency

User location report, search terms, conversion actions, call reports, networks, ad schedule and budget split. You get a short written findings note before anything changes.

Why: you see the problems in numbers and keep control of what goes to the client.
2

Lock spend to the real service area

Switch to presence only targeting, rebuild locations around the service ZIP codes or zones, exclude out of area cities, turn off Search Partners and Display, and set bid adjustments for the best converting zones.

Why: stops paying for clicks from people the client cannot serve.
3

Rebuild call tracking so only real leads count

Calls from ads and calls from the website counted only at 60 seconds or longer, short calls moved to secondary, call assets scheduled to business hours, and booked jobs imported back from the CRM or call tracking tool where available.

Why: bidding starts learning from qualified calls instead of junk.
4

Clean the queries and tighten the structure

Shared negative lists, service led ad groups on exact and phrase match, Dallas focused ad copy with service area and qualifiers in the text, and location assets linked to the Business Profile.

Why: fewer wrong searches, and the ad itself screens out people outside the area.
5

Optimize weekly and report to you, white label

Weekly search term and call quality review, budget moved toward the services and zones producing booked jobs, and a clean report your team can forward under your own brand.

Why: steady qualified leads, and you stay the face of the account.
