01  WHAT WE FOUND

A strong offer with no account management layer underneath it yet

ScalePaid has a sharp position: Reddit first, plus Google and ChatGPT, sold on full funnel ownership and proof before scale. The brief says media buyers own the platforms and nobody yet owns the client. On a month to month retainer, that gap is where clients churn. We also reviewed the public site and funnel on 25 November 2026.

HIGH IMPACT

1. Month to month clients need a story every month, and nobody owns it yet

Clients are told they can leave any month. The site promises attribution from first impression to closed deal, but there is no account manager, no reporting standard and no client health check. When reporting runs through busy media buyers, clients see platform metrics, not pipeline, and the month three renewal becomes a guess.

2. The agency site does not track the leads it sells for

We found no GA4, Google Tag Manager, Reddit Pixel or Meta Pixel on scalepaid.digital. Every call to action leaves the site for a third party form, so there is no record of which channel, article or partner sent each discovery call. For an agency that sells attribution, prospects who check will notice.

  • Sister brand links working

02  THE 30 DAY PLAN

Own the four clients first, then build the system around them

Each week ends with something the founder can see: client trust, a working report, a clean pipeline and a written playbook.

WEEK 1WEEK 2WEEK 3WEEK 4

Account management playbook
1

Week 1: Meet every client and audit every account

  • A kickoff call with each of the 4 clients covering ICP, offer, margins and what a win looks like
  • Access checklist for Reddit, Google, ChatGPT, analytics and CRM
  • Tracking audit: pixel, Conversions API, offline conversion imports
Why: clients who feel understood in week one stay through month three.
2

Week 2: Connect ad click to closed sale

  • Map each funnel step from ad to landing page to lead to deal
  • Task out pixel and CAPI fixes to the media buyers in ClickUp
  • CRM stages fed back so bidding can work toward qualified leads, not clicks
Why: the founder promises attribution, so every account needs it working.
3

Week 3: One reporting standard for every client

  • Looker Studio template: spend, CPM, CTR, cost per qualified lead, pipeline
  • A short monthly written story: what happened, why, and what comes next
  • Weekly client health score covering results, response time and sentiment
Why: a client who understands the numbers renews. One who just sees charts asks questions.
4

Week 4: Grow the accounts and write the playbook

  • One growth proposal per client, for example adding Google to a Reddit account
  • Creative request flow for designers and editors, with brief templates
  • Playbook covering onboarding, reporting, scope, change requests and billing
Why: the second account manager starts from day one, not from zero.

In parallel, in week one: tracking on scalepaid.digital, so the agency can see which channel sends its own discovery calls.
