1

Move online booking to BookedSmile so every confirmed booking reports back to Google Ads, GA4 and Meta

One booking route for Moseley, Barnt Green and Blythe Valley Park. Patients pick their site and book in three clicks, and each confirmed booking is counted as a conversion against the ad that produced it.

Why: bidding should learn from booked patients, not from clicks, and one system is easier for your team to run.
2

Google Search for each site: implants, cosmetic and new patients

Separate campaigns per site for dental implants, cosmetic dentistry and new patient appointments, with your awards, 564 reviews and Chrysalis Finance in the ad copy, each with its own cost per booked consultation target.

Why: these are the patients already searching near your sites, and today none of them see a paid ad from you.
3

Meta campaigns for cosmetic treatments, plus retargeting

Building on the ad that started on 21 October: cosmetic creative sent to a page with a clear booking route for each site, plus retargeting for people who visited but did not book.

Why: cosmetic patients compare for weeks, and the practice they keep seeing gets the booking.
4

SEO: a location page per site and a Google Business Profile for each of the three sites

A page each for Moseley, Barnt Green and Blythe Valley Park, local schema, and three Google Business Profiles that match those pages, so search and AI tools can describe each site correctly.

Why: organic enquiries cost nothing per click and keep coming when budgets change.
5

Follow-up automation and one weekly patient report

Instant reply by SMS or WhatsApp, matching the preference patients already choose on your contact form, then reminders and a rebook message for no-shows. One report: searches, clicks, booked consultations and treatment started, by channel and by site.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
03

BookedSmile: booking that reports back

BookedSmile is our own booking platform for dental practices. Patients book in three clicks, and every confirmed booking is sent server-side to Google Ads, GA4 and Meta, so your campaigns learn from real patients.

Confirmations and reminders go out by email and WhatsApp, and every patient gets an automatic Google review request after the visit. We set it up for you in a day and run your campaigns on top of it.
