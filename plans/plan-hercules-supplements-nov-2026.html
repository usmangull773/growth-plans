01 What we found

The ceiling is creative range, not budget

From the outside, the account looks like a brand that has found one strong winner and is leaning on it hard. That works until frequency climbs. After that, every extra dollar buys the same people seeing the same message, and CPA rises faster than revenue. Scaling from here means widening what the account says and who it says it to, while keeping the signal Meta optimises on clean.

High impact

Most of the spend story sits on one product and one angle

Of the 67 active ads we could see, 44 lead with Burn of the Gods or a weight loss message. Pre-workout, creatine, protein, gut health and bundles get a small share, mostly as discount ads. The weight loss buyer is a valuable audience, but it is one audience.

High impact

Many ads are versions of the same idea, not new concepts

Several ads share the same opening line ("30% off your first taste of our new drop", "The gods have delivered you 30% off"), and launches came in two batches, on 3 November and between 9 and 13 November. Copy variants of one concept fatigue together, so the account cannot find a second winner before the first one tires.

Running now

  • Same concept, new headline
  • Discount as the main hook
  • Batch launches, then wait
  • One buyer type: weight loss

What scales

  • 3 to 5 new concepts every week
  • Proof, founder and product angles carry the hook
  • Fixed weekly testing budget
  • Separate angles for performance, energy and gut health buyers
Medium impact

Profit is measured best on MER and new customer CPA, so the signal must hold

With 30% off sitewide running, platform ROAS can look healthy while margin per order shrinks. The account needs a CPA ceiling per product built from margin, read against blended MER from Shopify. That only works if Pixel and Conversions API purchases are deduplicated and matched well, so the first check is signal quality, not budget.

1

Audit the account and fix the signal

Check Pixel and CAPI deduplication, Purchase event match quality and Meta against Shopify order counts. Set a CPA ceiling per product from margin, and a weekly MER target.

Reason: scaling on a noisy signal spends more to learn the wrong thing.

2

Split the account into scale and test

One consolidated scaling campaign with broad targeting carries proven ads. One testing campaign with a fixed weekly budget proves new concepts before they get money. Post Black Friday, exclude recent buyers from prospecting.

Reason: winners keep their learning while tests stay cheap and readable.

3

Run a weekly creative testing rhythm with your creative team

Brief 3 to 5 new concepts each week, not new headlines. Angles to open first: the founder story, customer transformations backed by the 8,834 reviews, how Burn of the Gods works, pre-workout for performance buyers, and bundles for higher order value.

Reason: a second and third winner is what lets total spend rise without CPA rising with it.

4

Widen beyond one product

Give Energy of the Gods, Pump of the Gods, creatine and gut health their own buyer angles, and use bundles as the bridge from first order to repeat purchase.

Reason: more products with their own winners means more audiences to spend into.

5

Scale in steps and report on profit

Raise budgets on winners in measured steps while CPA stays under the ceiling. A weekly report shows spend, new customer CPA, MER and which concepts earned more budget.

Reason: decisions tied to MER grow profit, not just platform ROAS.
