1

Measure every enquiry route before spending

Form submissions, phone taps, WhatsApp taps and email clicks set up as conversions in GA4 and Google Ads. Then consultations booked and attended added from your secretary's diary as offline conversions.

Why: bidding should learn from booked consultations, not page views.
2

Make the site say the same thing everywhere

Add The Beaumont Hospital Bolton and Oaklands to the location lists, align the Pall Mall Court wording, fix the broken tummy tuck FAQ link, add mummy makeover to llms.txt, and check the structured data on each procedure page for physician and procedure details.

Why: Google and AI search trust consistent facts, and patients choose by nearest hospital.
3

Launch Google Search by procedure and hospital

Tummy tuck, mummy makeover and breast reduction each get their own campaign, ads and cost per consultation target, focused on Bolton, Manchester, Liverpool and Newton-le-Willows. A small brand campaign protects searches for your name.

Why: at £11,666 a mummy makeover, the searches you are missing are the valuable ones.
4

One consultation page per procedure

A starting price, consultation fee, choice of hospital, UK patient reviews and a short consultation request form above the fold on mobile, written within the GMC and CAP guidance your site already follows.

Why: a generic footer form makes a ready patient do extra work.
5

Fast follow-up and one weekly report

An instant acknowledgement to every enquiry, a reply target for your team, and reminders before consultations. Each week: enquiries, consultations booked, attended and surgery booked, by procedure and hospital. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
