03

BookedSmile: booking that reports back

BookedSmile is our own booking platform for dental practices. Patients book in three clicks, and every confirmed booking is sent server-side to Google Ads, GA4 and Meta, so your campaigns learn from real patients.

Confirmations and reminders go out by email and WhatsApp, and every patient gets an automatic Google review request after the visit. We set it up for you in a day and run your campaigns on top of it.
