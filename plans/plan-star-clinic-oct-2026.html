The 30 day plan

This is a growth setup for the whole patient journey, from the first search to a consultation in the diary. Show up for the procedures you are known for, make booking simple on the first visit, and feed every booked consultation back to Google so the budget keeps learning. Each step ends with one named action and a date to check it.

1

Google Search campaigns by procedure

Deep plane facelift, breast augmentation, Preservé and MIA, breast uplift and blepharoplasty each get their own ad group, ad copy that names Mr Marando, and a cost per consultation target. Brand searches are covered too, so nobody else sits above your name.

Why: you go from 0 ads to being present for the searches that lead to surgery.
2

A consultation landing page for paid traffic

One focused page per main procedure: surgeon credentials, recovery at a glance, guide price or finance note, Doctify reviews and a single "Book your consultation" action above the fold, fast on mobile.

Why: a long treatment page informs; a focused page books.
3

Pabau bookings tracked back to the ad and keyword

Capture the click ID and keyword when a patient lands, carry it into the Pabau booking, and import booked and attended consultations into Google Ads as offline conversions. Each procedure gets its own booking link so the source is never mixed.

Why: Google should optimise towards consultations booked, not clicks that left the site.
4

Automate the follow-up

Instant reply to every form enquiry, including evenings and weekends, consultation reminders by SMS and email, a rebook sequence for no-shows, and a gentle check-in for patients who had a consultation but have not yet booked surgery.

Why: the cheapest new patient is the enquiry you already paid for.
5

SEO, AEO and GEO fixes

Shorter procedure-first titles, British spelling in meta descriptions, a live llms.txt, schema checked on procedure and surgeon pages, FAQ answers on cost and recovery, and every "#" menu link turned into a real page or removed.

Why: Google and AI assistants recommend the clinic they can quote clearly.
