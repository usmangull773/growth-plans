Questions before you book

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your consultation with a Patient Journey Coordinator is free and there is no obligation to go ahead with any treatment.

Can I have my consultation virtually?

Yes. You can choose a face to face consultation at our MediaCityUK practice or a virtual consultation from home. Just pick your preference on the form.

How does finance work?

Treatments over £500 can be spread with 0% finance over 6 or 12 months, or extended finance over 24 or 36 months. Finance is subject to status, and your coordinator will explain the options and monthly costs before you decide.

How long does Invisalign take?

It depends on how much your teeth need to move. Some cases are quicker and others take longer, so your dentist will give you an expected timeline once your smile has been assessed.

What should I bring?

Bring a list of any medicines you take and any relevant medical history, plus questions about the treatments you are interested in. For a virtual consultation, a phone or laptop with a camera is all you need.
