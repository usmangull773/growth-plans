01 / WHAT WE FOUND

The product is strong. The measurement and the message are split across too many places.

You need someone to buy ads across channels, find where spend is wasted and report results clearly. Before any budget moves, three things decide whether that spend can be judged at all: whether a click can be followed to a paid plan, whether the offer is the same everywhere a buyer sees it, and whether the timing matches how tax firms buy software.

Finding 1: The ad click and the paid plan live on different domains

HIGH IMPACT

Consumers and pros land on taax.app (container GTM-TJKRHN29). Pros register and pay on taax.ai (container GTM-KNTLFXWV). pro.taax.app redirects to taax.ai, and affiliates sit on a fourth subdomain. Without cross domain linking and one shared conversion setup, Google and Meta see a click on one site and lose the sale on the other. They then optimise toward cheap trial signups instead of paying offices.

Finding 2: Three product names and two price lists in public

HIGH IMPACT

1

Audit every live account and rebuild tracking across all four domains

Review Google, Meta and Microsoft accounts, then set GA4 cross domain measurement, one consent ready tag plan, and separate events for trial start, account activated and paid plan.

Why: bidding can only improve once the platforms can see which clicks became paying offices.

2

Launch high intent Search for pros, built around your comparison pages

Tight campaigns for competitor alternatives, professional tax software, no EFIN options and practice management terms, each with its own budget cap and cost per trial ceiling.

Why: buyers who search a competitor's name are already in a buying cycle, and your $665 vs $2,700 page answers their main question.

3

Align the offer everywhere a buyer checks it

One product name in ads, one price list, the G2 listing updated, and a short trust block for tax data security on the landing pages.

Why: matching offers lift trial to paid conversion and keep ads clear of destination mismatch issues.

4

Retarget trial users and demo visitors on Meta and Google

Short sequences for people who started a trial but did not activate, and for visitors to pricing and compare pages, timed around the 7 day trial.

Why: the 7 day window is short in the busy season, and a reminder at day 3 and day 6 is cheaper than a new click.

5

Feed paid plans back to the platforms and shift budget weekly

Import paid subscriptions from the CRM as offline conversions, then move budget toward the campaigns and keywords producing paying offices, not just signups.

Why: optimising toward revenue is what turns ad spend into a predictable return.
