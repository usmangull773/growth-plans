1

Unify enquiries into one CRM view

Bookings from portal.dental, free consultations from Acuity, Tabeo finance enquiries, website forms and WhatsApp all land in one place, with the ad source and the clinic recorded on every enquiry, for Cheadle and Altrincham alike.

Why: you cannot move budget to what works until every enquiry carries its source.
2

Feed real outcomes back to Google and Meta

Import consultation attended and treatment started as offline conversions, so both platforms learn from patients rather than from clicks and form fills.

Why: with 63 Google ads and about 10 Meta ads live, bidding should favour the ads that produce treatment.
3

Treatment landing pages

Dedicated, fast mobile pages for implants, All-on-4, veneers, Invisalign and facial aesthetics, each with your price, Tabeo finance, reviews and the free online consultation above the fold.

Why: with All-on-4 from £8000 per arch and an implant and crown from £2700, each page only needs a few extra cases to pay for itself.
4

Automate the follow-up

Instant reply to every enquiry, reminders before consultations, a no-show rebook sequence, follow-up on treatment plans not yet started, and reactivation of past patients.

Why: the cheapest new patient is the enquiry you already paid for.
5

One weekly report from enquiry to treatment

Enquiries, booked consultations, attended and treatment started, by site and by channel. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions for each clinic, not a longer dashboard.
