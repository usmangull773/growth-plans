1

Set the baseline and fix the phone number

Search Console, GA4 and form tracking checked, with each lead tagged by state. One phone number across the site and the Google profile.

Reason: you want to pick a channel and go all in, so every lead has to show where it came from.

2

Clear the drafts out of Google's view

Redirect the duplicate reviews, team, pricing, home and story pages to one version each. Noindex the test and utility pages. Fix the homepage title.

Reason: authority stops being split across copies, so the real pages can rank.

3

Build three market pages

Custom Gym Shirts Florida, Custom Gym Shirts Texas, and Custom T-Shirts Lafayette and Louisiana. Each opens with a direct answer on price, minimums and turnaround, then shows real work and Design Lab concepts, with FAQ schema.

Reason: these are the exact searches in your three target markets, and right now no page answers them.

4

Add the AI search layer

Rewrite llms.txt by hand, add LocalBusiness and Service schema, and link the Murph, The Open and holiday category pages to the new state pages.

Reason: ChatGPT and AI Overviews pull from clear, structured answers. This is how UNITEE gets named when a gym owner asks for a recommendation.

5

Tighten the Lafayette profile

Categories, services, local event photos, and a steady review request flow from Louisiana schools, teams and events.

Reason: Maps is where Lafayette buyers look first, and it is the local half of the same SEO work.
