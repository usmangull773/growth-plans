01

What we found

I reviewed the live product pages, structured data and tracking tags on dogsy.co.uk, supersocks.co.uk and snugzy.com. All three run on Shopify and sell made to order, photo personalised gifts. That model works well on Google Shopping, but only when the feed tells Google exactly what each product is. Right now the feed and the tracking are doing less work than the products deserve.

High impact

Personalised products with no GTIN are being matched on thin titles

Custom products have no barcode, which is normal. It means Google decides where to show them almost entirely from the title, product type and Google category. The hero products carry short titles and 54 colour and size variants each, so impressions get split across near identical items.

High impact

Three accounts, three GA4 properties, and two brands selling the same product

Each store carries its own Google Ads tag and GA4 property, with tags hard coded on two stores and GTM on one. Super Socks and Dogsy both sell personalised dog socks at £18.99, so the two accounts can end up bidding against each other for the same shopper, and nobody sees a single number for group profit.

Medium impact

Missing review stars and a Christmas deadline problem

Dogsy socks show a 5.0 rating from 11 reviews in their product data. The Super Socks and Snugzy hero pages show no rating at all, even though both brands have Trustpilot profiles. Stars on Shopping ads lift click rate on gift searches. The other risk is timing: every item is made to order with 5 working day standard UK delivery, so the feed's shipping times and the ad copy must change as each Christmas cut off passes.

1

Audit all three accounts and fix purchase tracking

Check every conversion action, enhanced conversions and the Shopify Google channel on each store. Purchases report real order value, and the three brands use one naming standard.

Why: Smart Bidding can only find profit if it sees the right sale value.

2

Rebuild the feed with rules, not manual edits

Feed rules for titles (brand, product, personalisation, recipient, colour), Google product category, product type, identifier_exists, and custom labels for margin, best sellers and occasion.

Why: for products with no GTIN, the title and category decide which searches you can win.

3

Restructure Shopping and Performance Max around margin

Split hero products from the long tail, set target ROAS by margin tier, and add brand and cross brand negatives so Dogsy and Super Socks stop bidding against each other.

Why: the best sellers stop subsidising products that never convert.

4

Add reviews and Christmas messaging to the feed

Connect a product review feed, add Merchant Center promotions for Black Friday, and update shipping and handling times and ad copy as each Christmas order cut off passes.

Why: stars and a clear "order by" date lift click rate when gift shoppers compare.

5

Report one group number every week

A single weekly view across all three brands: spend, revenue, ROAS and best and worst products, with the next three actions written out.

Why: you see which brand deserves the next pound, not three separate reports.
