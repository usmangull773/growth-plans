1

Google Search campaigns by treatment

Implants, Invisalign, veneers and composite bonding each get their own ad group, ad copy and cost per consultation target, using your late opening hours, weekend appointments and 4.9 rating in the ads.

Why: at £2500 to £9000 per implant case, the searches you are missing are the most valuable ones.
2

A fast landing page for each treatment

One page per treatment with a keyword H1, real "from" prices, a finance option, a free consultation offer, reviews and a short form above the fold, built to score 80 or higher on mobile. A sample implant landing page is included with this plan.

Why: a patient weighing a large treatment needs a monthly figure and an easy first step.
3

SEO basics and image weight

Add one keyword H1 and a written meta description to every key page, compress and lazy load the 272 homepage images, link bonding and sedation from the navigation, and add an llms.txt file for AI search.

Why: these are quick fixes that help both organic rankings and paid landing page quality.
4

Automate the follow-up

Instant reply to every enquiry and WhatsApp message, SMS and email reminders before consultations, a no-show rebook sequence, and a nudge for treatment plans not yet started. Membership patients get offered a cosmetic consultation.

Why: the cheapest new patient is the enquiry you already paid for.
5

One weekly report from enquiry to treatment

Enquiries, booked consultations, attended and treatment started, by treatment and by channel, with consultation and treatment outcomes fed back to Google and Meta as conversions. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
