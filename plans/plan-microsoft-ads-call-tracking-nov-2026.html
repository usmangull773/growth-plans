01

What we found

From your brief: two campaigns, two WordPress sites, and the only conversion that matters is a phone call. Right now Microsoft Ads cannot see those calls, so it has no way to tell a good click from a wasted one.

HIGH IMPACT

No call conversions means the account is bidding blind

Without a conversion goal, Microsoft Ads reports clicks and cost, but not results. You cannot tell which campaign, keyword or site is producing calls, and automated bidding has no signal to optimize toward.

Today

  • Clicks and spend only
  • No idea which site gets the calls
  • Keywords judged on clicks, not calls
  • Smart bidding has nothing to learn from

After setup

  • Calls shown per campaign and keyword
  • Cost per call visible for each site
  • Budget moves to what rings the phone
  • Conversion based bidding becomes possible
HIGH IMPACT

Calls come through two different doors, and each needs its own setup

A call placed straight from the ad and a call placed after someone lands on the website are tracked in different ways. Both have to be covered, or part of your results stays invisible.

Two sites in one account can blur the numbers

If both sites share one goal, a call from site A can look like a result for site B. Each site gets its own tag and its own named goal, so the reports never mix.

1

Audit what is already there

Check both sites and the account for existing UET tags, goals, call assets and the phone numbers shown in the header, footer and contact pages.

Why: avoids duplicate tags and double counted calls.
2

Install a UET tag on each WordPress site

Through Google Tag Manager, or a header plugin if GTM is not in use. Confirmed with the UET Tag Helper on desktop and mobile.

Why: the tag is what lets Microsoft Ads see what happens after the click.
3

Create one phone call goal per site

A custom event goal that fires on every phone link tap, counted once per click and named by site, so each campaign reports its own calls.

Why: keeps site A and site B results cleanly apart.
4

Set up call assets with call tracking on both campaigns

Call assets with Microsoft forwarding numbers, scheduled to your business hours, so calls placed straight from the ad are logged.

Why: covers the people who never visit the website at all.
5

Test every path and hand over a short guide

Live test calls from the ad and from each site on mobile, confirmed in the reports, plus a one page note showing exactly where to see your calls.

Why: you get proof it works, not just a promise.
