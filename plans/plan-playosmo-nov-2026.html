01

What we found

From your brief and a review of the public storefront, the core issue is that Google Ads cannot be optimized until it is counting the right purchases. Shopping and Performance Max bid on whatever conversion signal they receive, so tracking comes first, then the feed, then testing.

High impact

Tracking: the same sale can be counted more than once, or not at all

Shopify stores commonly run the Google and YouTube app, a GA4 tag and a separate Google Ads tag at the same time. When two of them fire on checkout, Performance Max sees inflated ROAS and keeps spending on the wrong products. When none of them carry order value correctly, the account bids blind.

What usually breaksWhat we set up
Two purchase actions marked Primary in Google AdsOne Primary purchase action, the rest set to Secondary
Purchase value missing or sent without currencyOrder value, currency and transaction ID on every purchase
No deduplication, so refreshes count twiceTransaction ID dedupe plus Enhanced Conversions
GA4 and Shopify totals never reconciledWeekly check: Shopify orders vs GA4 vs Google Ads
High impact

Shopping feed: product titles are too short to match how parents search

Titles such as "Genius Numbers - Fire" make sense on the site, but in Google Shopping they miss the words parents type: the subject, the age and the device. Google matches Shopping ads mostly from the title, so richer feed titles reach more of the right searches without changing the storefront.

The same audit covers Merchant Center diagnostics, GTIN and brand fields, item group IDs for the iPad and Fire versions, and custom labels so best sellers and bundles get their own budgets.

Medium impact

A/B testing: tests need a clean window and a single success metric

With a 25% sitewide sale live, any landing page test started this month would mostly measure the discount. The right setup is ready to launch now and read once traffic returns to normal pricing, judged on purchase conversion rate and ROAS from the same tracked source.

1

Audit and fix Google Ads, GA4 and Shopify tracking

Map every tag firing on checkout, keep one Primary purchase action with value, currency and transaction ID, turn on Enhanced Conversions, and reconcile Shopify orders against GA4 and Google Ads.

Why: Shopping and PMax bidding is only as good as the purchase signal behind it.
2

Merchant Center and product feed audit

Clear disapprovals and warnings, rewrite feed titles with subject, age and device, fill GTIN, brand and product type, group iPad and Fire versions, and add custom labels for best sellers, bundles and margin.

Why: the feed decides which searches your products can show for.
3

Restructure Shopping, Performance Max and Search

Split PMax by custom label so hero kits get their own budget, protect brand Search, and add negatives so Shopping stops paying for low intent queries.

Why: budget follows the products that actually return a profit.
4

Build the landing page A/B test

Control vs one variant (for example the starter kit page with a device picker above the fold), traffic split evenly, success measured on purchase conversion rate and ROAS, launched after the sale window closes.

Why: a clean test gives a decision you can trust and roll out.
5

Report and next 60 days

A clear view of spend, revenue and ROAS by campaign and product group, plus the list of changes for the next two months.

Why: you see exactly where the money goes and what it returns.
