01

What we found

We reviewed the public xilo.io site and its live tag manager container. The ads part of this job is small. The tracking part decides whether the test teaches you anything.

Why you posted

You want a small, measurable paid program: protect your brand searches, take some competitor searches, and bring site visitors back on Meta and LinkedIn, with a cost per lead you can trust each month.

The problem

Paid spend cannot be judged on cost per demo if the conversion data counts button clicks, and LinkedIn retargeting cannot start until there is an Insight Tag and a visitor audience to retarget.

Root cause

The tag setup dates from the older site. Triggers are tied to button clicks and old button classes, a retired Universal Analytics tag is still in the container, and LinkedIn was never added.

1. Demo conversions look tied to button clicks, not completed demo forms

High impact

In the container, the Google Ads conversion tags and the Meta "Scheduled Demo" event are triggered by clicks on elements like the demo CTA button and "SCHEDULE A DEMO" text. A click on "Request Demo" is not a demo request. Google's bidding then learns from people who clicked, including people who left the form empty. Several triggers also point at button classes from the earlier site design, so after the redesign some may not fire at all.

Now
  • Conversion counted on CTA click
  • Meta event is a custom click event, "Scheduled Demo"
  • Enhanced conversions off
  • No signup conversion for new accounts
After week 1
  • Conversion counted only on a successful demo form submit
  • Meta standard "Lead" event on the same submit, plus "CompleteRegistration" for signups
  • Enhanced conversions on, using the form email
  • Demo request and signup reported separately

2. Tag inventory: LinkedIn is missing, GA4 is not in the container

High impact

Your job asks for Meta and LinkedIn retargeting. Meta is ready to build on. LinkedIn has no Insight Tag yet, so there is no visitor audience on LinkedIn today. Audiences need a few weeks to fill, so this tag should go live on day one.

3. Existing customers will eat the retargeting budget unless they are excluded

Medium impact

XILO serves 500+ agencies, and those users visit xilo.io to reach the login page. A plain "all website visitors" audience will spend a share of a small budget showing demo ads to people who already pay you. The fix is simple: exclude login page visitors and existing converters on all three platforms.

The 30 day plan

Tracking first, then Search, then retargeting once audiences have started filling. Lean budgets, clear caps.

1
Week 1

Fix the conversion signal

Demo request and signup tracked on successful submit across Google Ads, Meta and LinkedIn. Enhanced conversions on. LinkedIn Insight Tag live. Retired UA tag removed. GA4 confirmed or added.

Why: every later decision depends on this being right.

2
Week 2

Brand Search campaign

"XILO" and variations on exact and phrase match, sitelinks to the AI Intake, Quoting and Proposal pages, negatives for login, careers, support and unrelated "xilo" searches.

Why: cheap clicks that stop competitors and resellers from taking people already looking for you.

3
Week 2

Competitor Search campaign

One ad group per competitor, comparison angle copy, own daily cap, judged on cost per demo request only.

Why: small volume, expensive clicks. It needs its own budget so it never starves the brand campaign.

4
Week 3

Meta and LinkedIn retargeting

2 to 3 ad variations per platform from your creative. Demo page visitors and product page visitors, with customers and converters excluded. Frequency capped.

Why: B2B buyers rarely book on the first visit. This brings them back at low cost.

5
Week 4

First report and first cuts

One page monthly report: spend, clicks, conversions and cost per lead by campaign and platform. Search terms reviewed, weak competitor terms paused.

Why: you asked for a simple report, not a deck. That is what you get.

Approach to competitor keyword campaigns

Each competitor gets its own ad group on exact and phrase match for its brand name plus "alternative" and "vs" searches, with ad copy that leads on what XILO does differently (one AI agent across intake, quoting and proposals) and never uses their trademark in the ad text. Each one runs under a hard daily cap and a cost per demo ceiling, and any term that spends past that ceiling without a demo request is paused. Brand campaign budget is kept separate so competitor tests never take money from protecting your own name.
