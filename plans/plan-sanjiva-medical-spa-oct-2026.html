1

Connect every booking and consultation outcome back to Google Ads and Meta on your existing booking system

Bookings stay on PatientNow. We add the Meta pixel, report each booked appointment and consultation outcome to Google Ads, GA4 and Meta, and move the site to one tracked phone number so every call is counted.

Why: bidding should learn from booked patients, not from clicks.
2

Google Search for Botox, Emsculpt Neo and medical weight loss in Dallas

Separate campaigns for each treatment, with your 5.0 rating, board-certified MD ownership and the 20% first-visit offer in the ad copy, each with its own cost per booking target.

Why: these are the patients already looking for what you offer.
3

Meta and Instagram campaigns for memberships and weight loss

Short video and treatment creative for the Platinum membership and semaglutide and tirzepatide programs, plus retargeting for people who visited but did not book.

Why: aesthetics patients compare for weeks, and the practice they keep seeing gets the booking.
4

SEO and Google Business Profile: treatment pages and a cleaner site

Dedicated pages for weight loss, Emsculpt Neo and Botox, one phone number across the site, a fixed footer and Services link, local schema and a Google Business Profile that matches your treatment pages.

Why: organic inquiries cost nothing per click and keep coming when budgets change.
5

Follow-up automation and one weekly patient report

Instant reply, reminders and a rebook message for no-shows, then one report: searches, clicks, calls, bookings and treatments started, by channel.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
