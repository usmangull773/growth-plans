What we found

Your case studies show strong client work: a dealership at $23 per lead, an aesthetics clinic at 7.8x ROAS, a roofing store scaled to 4x ROAS. The gap is on your own site. The page that sells your agency is not measuring the leads it produces. I checked the live site in a browser, read the network requests and scanned the site bundle for tag IDs.

1. No analytics or ad tracking on marketingnova.be

High impact

The site loads no Google Tag Manager container, no GA4 property, no Google Ads conversion tag and no Meta Pixel. The only measurement running is the site builder's built-in page counter. You cannot see which channel or page produced a strategy call, and any future campaign for your own agency would have nothing to optimise toward.
