1

Move online booking to BookedSmile so every confirmed booking and attended treatment reports back to Google Ads, GA4 and Meta

Keep the 2 minute callback for patients who want it, and add real online booking to every offer page so a patient can choose a consultation time in three clicks. Each confirmed booking, and later each attended visit, is counted as a conversion.

Why: with around 99 live ads, bidding should learn from patients who arrive, not from form fills alone.
2

One record per patient across every route

Offer page forms, WhatsApp, the "Meta" SMS keyword, the Online Evaluation form and calls tagged by source ad and treatment, with five stages: inquiry, first reply, booked consultation, attended, treatment started.

Why: the same patient often clicks a Meta ad, texts, then calls, and only one route gets the credit today.
3

Judge every ad by cost per attended patient

Once tracking settles, review the 55 Google ads and around 44 Meta results by treatment. Pause or rewrite those with no booked patients behind them, and move budget toward the treatments and offers with the lowest cost per attended consultation.

Why: at your volume, the gain is in cutting what does not convert, not in adding more ads.
4

Travel route, follow up and search readability

A country field next to state, a WhatsApp first reply for out of state and international inquiries, reminders for anyone not booked within 48 hours in English and Spanish, a clean llms.txt and written meta descriptions on key pages.

Why: a patient flying in for veneers compares for weeks, and the practice that keeps replying gets the booking.
5

One weekly patient report

Inquiries, first reply time, booked consultations, attended visits and treatments started, by treatment and by channel. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
