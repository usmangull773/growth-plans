1

Meta campaigns for facial aesthetics and cosmetic dentistry

New campaigns for Botox, fillers, Profhilo, bonding and Invisalign, with the pixel and Conversions API checked first and retargeting for visitors who viewed a treatment page but did not book.

Why: these treatments sell on Instagram and Facebook, and you are running about 3 ads there against 65 on Google.
2

One clear consultation offer

Choose free or £75 for cosmetic consultations and use the same wording on the fees page, the welcome offer page, every treatment page and every ad.

Why: a patient who sees two prices hesitates, and hesitation loses the booking.
3

Treatment landing pages

Dedicated, fast mobile pages for Botox and fillers, Profhilo, bonding and Invisalign, each with your price, Tabeo finance, reviews and the consultation offer above the fold.

Why: with Invisalign from £2500 and porcelain veneers at £900 per tooth, a page that converts better pays back quickly.
4

Automate the follow-up

Instant reply to every enquiry, reminders before consultations, a no-show rebook sequence, follow-up on treatment plans not yet started, and rebook reminders for injectables every few months.

Why: injectables are repeat treatments, so a timely reminder brings the same patient back.
5

One weekly report from enquiry to treatment

Enquiries, booked consultations, attended and treatment started, by treatment and by channel. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
