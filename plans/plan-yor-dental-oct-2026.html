1

Build the patient enquiry route

A short patient consultation form on a fast landing page, online booking for the free consultation, and an instant reply by SMS and email. Book a consultation and every paid ad point here, and the referral form stays on its own page for dentists. Prices, plan fee and phone number are made consistent, and the finance quote link is fixed.

Why: your 27 Google ads are paying for clicks that currently have nowhere to enquire.
2

Track real outcomes in Google, Meta and GA4

Consultation form submits, online bookings and calls from ads become conversions through your existing GTM container, then consultation attended and treatment started are imported as offline conversions.

Why: bidding should learn from patients who start treatment, not from page views.
3

Launch Meta for Invisalign and bonding

Short video and review led ads for Invisalign from £2,200 and composite bonding from £1,575, each with the monthly finance figure, sending people to the consultation page. Retarget visitors who looked at a treatment but did not enquire.

Why: you run 1 Meta ad while nearby practices run 10 to 56.
4

Automate the follow-up

Instant reply to every enquiry, reminders before consultations, a no-show rebook sequence, and a nudge for treatment plans not yet started. Your email list gets a finance and free consultation message, and patients get offered YOR Skin where it fits.

Why: the cheapest new patient is the enquiry you already paid for.
5

One weekly report from enquiry to treatment

Enquiries, booked consultations, attended and treatment started, by treatment and by channel. Where a number cannot be measured yet, the report says so.

Why: you get decisions, not a longer dashboard.
