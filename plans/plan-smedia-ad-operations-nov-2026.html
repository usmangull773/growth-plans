01

What we found

sMedia sells dealers two things: transparency and zero wasted spend. Both depend on the execution layer behind them. This role is where one wrong budget, one missing pixel or one stale offer lands in a dealer's read only report. The plan below is built around closing those gaps.

High impact

Ticket speed across 350+ rooftops creates repeat error patterns

The daily queue covers budget adjustments, ad copy updates, geo tweaks and creative swaps on four platforms. Most QA failures in this kind of work come from the same few slips. Each ticket type needs a fixed check before it is marked done.

Ticket typeCommon slipCheck before submit
Budget adjustmentCampaign budget changed when a shared budget or monthly cap controls spendConfirm budget scope and remaining monthly spend, note before and after values
Ad copy / offer updateOld offer still live in another ad group or on MicrosoftSearch all accounts for the old offer text, replace every instance
Geo targeting"Presence or interest" left on, radius overlaps a sister storeSet to presence, check the radius on the map against the rooftop
Creative swapNew creative approved but the old ad keeps spendingPause the old ad, confirm delivery on the new one the same day
High impact

Tracking is the product the dealer actually sees

Your campaigns optimize to three metrics: Engaged Prospect Metric (30+ seconds on a VDP), click to call, and completed forms, all inside a clean GA4 property per client. A pixel or event missed at launch shows up as zero in the dealer's view, and the transparency promise breaks on day one.

ItemLaunch on assumptionLaunch on verification
GA4 key eventsAssumed firingTested in DebugView with a live VDP session
Meta pixel and CAPIPixel present, events uncheckedTest events confirmed for ViewContent, Lead and Contact
TikTok pixelInstalled, never testedPixel Helper plus test events before go live
Click to callNumber in ad, no testTest call logged as a conversion before handover
Medium impact

Feed driven campaigns and month end pacing are where spend drifts

VLA, Meta AIA, TikTok AIA and PMax by model all run off the daily inventory scraper. Feed disapprovals (price mismatch, missing VIN, image issues) quietly cut delivery, and co-op or monthly budgets drift if pacing is only checked near month end. A daily sweep against fixed thresholds catches both early.

Day of a 30 day monthExpected spendFlag ifAction
Day 1033%Under 25% or over 40%Adjust daily budget, note in the ticket

Day 2893%Under 88% or over 98%Set soft caps for the final days
