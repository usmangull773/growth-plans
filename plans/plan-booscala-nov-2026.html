01

What we found

You already run Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop and Instagram for K-Beauty brands, and your Amazon side is mature. What you are asking for is different from a fix list: a clear audit of one Meta account plus the reasoning behind each call, so the next decision is made in house with confidence.

Amazon logic does not carry over to Meta

High impact

A team trained on Amazon PPC is used to buying intent: keywords, bids, search terms. Meta works the other way. The creative is the targeting, the algorithm needs clean purchase signals, and short term ROAS swings are normal while it learns. Most Meta accounts that underperform for marketplace teams are being managed with search habits.

Targeting does the work

Optimising for clicks or add to cart
Optimising for purchase with value passed back

Decisions are only as good as the purchase signal

High impact

With Shopify as the checkout, the first question in any Meta audit is whether Meta sees the sales that really happen. If Pixel and Conversions API are not deduplicated, or match quality is low, every other recommendation sits on bad data. This is checked first, before any budget or creative opinion.

Your creative engine is ready, Meta needs a testing system for it

Medium impact

You already produce AI video, UGC and creator content for K-Beauty. That is the hardest part of Meta solved. What usually goes missing is a simple rule set for testing it: how many angles per round, how long to run them, and which signal retires an ad. K-Beauty sells on ingredients, routines and visible results, so testing by angle beats testing by format.

1

Tracking and data check

Pixel, CAPI, deduplication, match quality and Meta sales against Shopify orders. You get a short note on how far the numbers can be trusted.

Every later decision depends on this.
2

Live audit, session one: structure and spend

We walk the account together on screen share. Campaign layout, budget split, learning status, audience setup. For every issue I explain what I see, why it costs money, and what I would change.

You learn the reasoning, not only the fix.
3

Live audit, session two: creative and funnel

Top and bottom ads by hook rate, hold rate, CTR and cost per purchase, then the product pages they send traffic to. We agree a test plan by angle for your K-Beauty products.

Creative is where Meta results are won.
4

Your team makes the changes, I review

Your team applies the priority changes. I check the edits and flag anything that would reset learning or split budget too thin.

Builds in house confidence on real decisions.
5

Session three: readout and decision playbook

We review what moved, then hand over a one page playbook: weekly checks, scaling rules, when to kill an ad, and what to ignore.

The audit becomes a repeatable routine.
