01

What we found

Your brief is clear: you already have accounts, landing pages and sales processes. The gap is lead quality and CPL stability across multiple local accounts. These are the three issues that usually sit behind that, plus one quick note from haikusteps.com.

High impact

1. Instant Forms are optimising for volume, not for patients who show up

Instant Forms almost always produce the lowest CPL in the account. Left on the default "More volume" setting, they also produce the most wrong numbers, tyre kickers and people who never answer the follow-up call. If the algorithm only ever sees "lead submitted", it keeps finding more people who submit and do nothing else.

Typical setup

  • More volume form type
  • Name, email, phone only
  • Optimises to form submit
  • CRM outcome never sent back

What works

  • Higher intent form with review screen
  • 1 to 2 qualifying questions (service, timing, location)
  • Conversion Leads optimisation
  • Booked and showed sent back via CAPI
High impact

2. Pixel and Conversions API signals need to match before results can be trusted

You listed Pixel and CAPI troubleshooting in the brief, which usually means something is already off. The common faults on local service accounts are duplicate leads (browser and server events without a shared event ID), low Event Match Quality, and website forms that fire on page load instead of on a real submit.

Medium impact

3. CPL spikes need a fixed diagnosis order, not guesswork

When CPL jumps 50%, the instinct is to change bids, audiences and creative all at once. That resets learning and hides the real cause. Each account needs the same five checks, run in order, so the fix is the smallest one that works.

Quick win

Spotted on haikusteps.com

The "Business Resources" block on the homepage still shows placeholder Lorem ipsum text, and three blog titles carry an internal "FP-" prefix. Both are five minute fixes, worth doing before you send ad traffic or prospects to the site.

02

The 30 day plan

Built for one account first, then rolled out as a repeatable setup for every client account you add.

Week by week

Week 1
Audit and tracking
Week 2
Form and structure
Week 3
Creative tests
Week 4
Scale and report
1

Audit the account and fix the signal

Check Pixel and CAPI deduplication, Event Match Quality and form triggers. Confirm every lead reaches the CRM and the sales team.

Why: every later decision depends on lead counts you can trust.

2

Rebuild Instant Forms for intent

Switch to higher intent forms with a review screen and one or two qualifying questions per service. Keep a website conversion campaign running beside it to compare lead quality, not just CPL.

Why: a slightly higher CPL with more booked appointments is the cheaper lead.

3

Send booked outcomes back to Meta

Map CRM stages (contacted, booked, showed) to CAPI events and move eligible campaigns to Conversion Leads optimisation.

Why: Meta finds more people like the ones who actually book.

4

Run structured creative tests

Three angles per service (offer, proof, problem), tested one variable at a time with a fixed spend and a fixed read date.

Why: fresh creative is the main defence against fatigue and rising CPL.

5

Scale winners and report weekly

Raise budgets in 15 to 20% steps on ad sets holding CPL, with a CPL ceiling agreed per client. A short weekly report: spend, leads, CPL, booked rate, and next actions.

Why: steady steps keep learning stable, and clear reports save you time.
