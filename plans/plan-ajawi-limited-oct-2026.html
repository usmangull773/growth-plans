01

What we found

Your buyers live in New York, South Florida, Toronto and London, but the house gets built in Jamaica. That split is the single biggest source of wasted spend in accounts like yours, because Google treats location, search intent and conversions very differently when the person searching and the project are in two countries. Below are the three issues I expect to confirm first, based on your brief and your business model. Each one gets checked against your live data before it goes into the final report.

High impact

1. Location settings are likely pulling the wrong people

If campaigns target Jamaica, you mostly reach people who already live there and can visit a contractor in person. If they target the US or UK broadly with "presence or interest", you pay for searches from people with no link to Jamaica at all. Neither setting finds the overseas Jamaican planning a home back on the island.

High impact

2. Search terms leaking into jobs, DIY and free plans

Construction and design keywords on broad or phrase match attract a predictable set of searches that will never become a client. The audit lists every term like this from your search terms report, the campaign it ran in, and the exact negative to add at account or campaign level.

Terms that waste spend

  • construction jobs jamaica
  • free house plans jamaica
  • building materials price list
  • how to build a house yourself
  • architect salary jamaica
  • house for rent kingston

Terms that bring clients

  • build a house in jamaica from abroad
  • contractor in jamaica for overseas owner
  • house plans jamaica returning resident
  • construction manager st catherine
  • build home in jamaica while living in usa
  • jamaica home builder for diaspora
Medium impact

3. Conversions probably count clicks, not consultations

A design and build project is a high value, slow decision. If the account counts page views, button clicks or every form fill as a conversion, Smart Bidding optimizes toward cheap curiosity instead of owners who book a consultation.

Conversion actionLikely statusRecommendation
Page view or button clickPrimaryMove to secondary, observe only
Contact form submitPrimaryKeep, check for duplicate firing
Calls and WhatsApp clicksNot trackedAdd, with minimum call duration
Qualified consultationNot trackedImport offline from your lead list, make it the bidding goal

1

Read-only review of all nine areas

Structure, settings, keywords and match types, search terms, ad copy, geo and demographics, audiences, wasted spend, and strategic gaps.

Why: every recommendation is tied to a real campaign, ad group or search term in your account.
2

Fix location and keyword intent

Switch to presence only, split diaspora and local campaigns, and add the full negative list by campaign.

Why: this stops the largest leak first and costs nothing to change.
3

Rebuild conversion goals

Demote soft actions, add call and WhatsApp tracking, set up offline import of qualified consultations.

Why: bidding can only improve once it is pointed at real enquiries.
4

Rewrite ads for the overseas owner

Lead with what a buyer abroad worries about: remote progress reports, construction administration, design included, a fixed point of contact in Jamaica.

Why: generic builder copy does not answer the trust question someone 2,000 miles away is asking.
5

Test list for later

Customer match from past enquiries, remarketing to site visitors, and a separate campaign for each top diaspora city.

Why: these need clean tracking and a few weeks of data before they are worth the spend.
