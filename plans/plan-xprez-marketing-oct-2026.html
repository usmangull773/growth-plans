01

What we found

Your brief describes three separate breaks that show up as one symptom: leads exist in WhatConverts but Google Ads cannot see them or bid on them. Each one has a known cause and a test that proves it.

Click IDs are captured but never become Google Ads conversions

High impact

A gclid in WhatConverts proves the click was captured. A zero in Google Ads after 72 hours means the import step is failing or the result is reported somewhere you are not looking. This is the order we check it in, cheapest test first.

#CheckpointWhat we look for
1Date range and columnImported conversions land on the click date, not the upload date. A secondary action only shows in All conversions.
2Google Ads upload diagnosticsErrors such as click not found, click too old, conversion action not found, or an action created too recently to accept uploads.
3WhatConverts integrationCorrect Google Ads customer ID, a valid connection, the exact conversion action name mapped, and lead filters that may be holding the lead back.
4Click ID ownershipA gclid from a different account or the manager account, or a wbraid or gbraid that the import path does not accept.
5Conversion action settingsSource set to click imports, status active, counting and conversion window correct, primary or secondary set on purpose.

Two scripts compete for the same phone number

High impact

When a landing page shows a Google forwarding number instead of the WhatConverts pool number, Google's website call tag and the WhatConverts script are both swapping the same number, and whichever loads last wins. We confirm it in GTM Preview and the rendered page, not the page source.

When it breaksWhen it is fixed
Who swaps the numberGoogle call tag and WhatConverts both target itWhatConverts only, Google call tag removed or pointed elsewhere
Where tags liveHard coded in the theme and also in GTMOne GTM container, one owner per tag
Load rulesFires on every page, no exceptionsTriggers with exceptions, tested in Preview
Page cacheSwapped number cached and served to everyoneSwap runs client side after cache
Result in Google AdsCalls split or counted twiceOne call, one source, one imported conversion

Calls from the ad call button can skip WhatConverts

Medium impact

If the call asset holds the business line, Google's forwarding number rings the client directly and WhatConverts never hears the call. Test calls also vanish when an account level asset overrides the campaign one, or when the spam challenge drops the tester before the call connects.

1
Week 1, days 1 to 3

Trace one lead from click to CRM

  • Run a real ad click, a real call and a real Gravity Forms submission on the pilot account.
  • Record the gclid, wbraid or gbraid at every hop: landing page, WhatConverts, Google Ads upload log, GA4, CRM.
  • Run the five checkpoint order above on the 72 hour zero conversion issue.
Why: one written trace shows exactly where the lead stops, so fixes are based on evidence, not guesses.
2
Weeks 1 and 2

Rebuild the Google Ads conversion layer

  • Confirm auto-tagging is on and the tracking template passes the click ID through untouched.
  • Set up click based import actions for qualified calls and qualified forms, plus call extension conversions with a minimum call length.
  • Turn on enhanced conversions for leads as a backup match when the click ID is missing.
  • Set primary versus secondary so bidding only learns from leads that matter.
Why: Smart Bidding can only optimize toward what is imported as primary. Wrong settings mean the budget chases the wrong signal.
3
Week 2

Number pools and call flows

  • Dynamic pools sized to peak traffic on landing pages, plus dedicated numbers for call assets, Local Services Ads, Google Business Profile and offline.
  • Call flows built and tested: dial steps, ring timing, voicemail, recording disclosure, spam challenge and caller ID.
  • Carrier forwarding or number port coordinated where a client keeps a legacy line.
Why: every number source gets its own attribution, and no real customer gets blocked by a spam rule.
4
Weeks 2 and 3

Forms and Google Tag Manager

  • Register each Gravity Forms form, including multi step forms, so every submission becomes a WhatConverts lead with source and click ID.
  • Load the WhatConverts script through GTM with page triggers and exceptions, and remove any conflicting Google call tag.
  • Test in Preview, publish with named versions so any change can be rolled back.
Why: forms are where click IDs most often go missing, and clean GTM versioning stops the next edit from breaking tracking.
5
Weeks 3 and 4

Reconcile counts and write the SOP

  • Weekly count match across WhatConverts, Google Ads, GA4 and the CRM, with each gap explained: spam filtered, attribution window, duplicate, or lead with no click.
  • Written SOP with screenshots and a go live checklist, then a second client account rolled out from it.
Why: your team can repeat the setup without me, and clients see numbers that agree.
