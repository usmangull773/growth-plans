1

Launch a consultation landing page

One fast page on gormanplasticsurgery.com: who Mr Gorman is, the procedures he performs, where he consults, what happens at a consultation, review scores and a short enquiry form. It replaces the coming soon page while the full site is finished, and the Doctify Book button starts working on day one.

Why: every review, search and referral already points here.
2

Claim and complete the Google Business Profile

Practice name, Hale and Pines Hospital locations, procedure list, photos, hours and a link to the new consultation page, with a simple routine for asking happy patients to leave a Google review.

Why: a name search shows the profile before any website.
3

Track every enquiry to consultation booked

Form submissions, calls from the page and WhatsApp clicks recorded in GA4 and Google Ads. Consultation booked and surgery booked imported as offline conversions from your diary.

Why: bidding should learn from booked consultations, not clicks.
4

Brand and procedure Search campaigns

A low cost Brand campaign so patients searching "Mark Gorman" or "Gorman Plastic Surgery" always reach the right page, then breast augmentation, breast reduction, breast uplift, tummy tuck and mummy makeover searches in Manchester, Hale and Altrincham.

Why: patients searching your name have already chosen you.
5

Automate the follow-up

Instant reply to every enquiry, a reminder before the consultation, a check in after it, and a gentle nudge for patients who are still deciding. Written in your voice and approved by you.

Why: the cheapest new patient is the enquiry you already have.
6

SEO and AI search basics

A valid robots.txt and sitemap, Physician and MedicalClinic schema with review scores, an llms.txt file, and clear answers to the questions patients ask, so Google and AI assistants describe Mr Gorman correctly.

Why: patients now ask AI tools who to see, and they read your site to answer.
